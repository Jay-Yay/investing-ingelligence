### Bank of America S&P 500 outlook: 7,800 in 2027

(https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/25/bank-of-america-sp-500-outlook.html?taid=6b06d09b5f9bc30001811b2c&utm_campaign=trueanthem&utm_content=main&utm_medium=social&utm_source=twitter)

The stock market will have to make up some ground in 2027, according to Bank of America. Savita Subramanian, the firm's head of U.S. equity strategy, expects the S & P 500 will climb to 7,800 by the end of next year. The target implies just an 8.3% climb from Tuesday's close that the strategist said represents a better entry point for investors after this month's corrective action. It's also a level that the S & P 500 had already reached. The broader index first topped 7,800 in October, before a rapid succession of developments in November — including the exit and then reinstatement of Jensen Huang as Nvidia's CEO, and the Democratic sweep of Congress during the midterm elections — finally cracked a stock market that's been sputtering for roughly half a year. The strategist expects that the choppy trading action could continue next year, as supply and demand dynamics deteriorate in the market and the question around AI sustainability continues to weigh on investors. .SPX YTD mountain SPX year to date "What keeps us worried?" she wrote Tuesday. "Worsening supply (IPOs/issuance) vs. demand (waning buybacks, aging demographics suggesting risk of retirement fund outflows). Fading earnings/margin visibility and questions around the durability of the AI investment cycle plague us, and high expectations plus, until recently, S & P index complacency ('Don't risk it with single stocks, just buy the index') increase downside risk." The conflicting cross currents in the market also implies a wide range of outcomes. The strategist expects that the S & P 500 can rise to 8,500 in her bull case, and fall to 6,600 in her bear case. For the bulls, a neutral sentiment reading in the firm's sell side indicator implies double-digit equity returns. For the bears, a poor reading in the firm's earnings surprise indicator suggests earnings next year could disappoint investors, relative to expectations. Still, there remain buying opportunities. The strategist highlighted communication services as a buying opportunity, raising the sector to overweight. She also expects AI adopters such as financials and health care will outperform. One contrarian take she had was a switch to overweight consumer discretionary and underweight staples, thanks to an overdue housing turnover cycle that should mean a rise in discretionary spending as Americans make home purchases. That could help investors wade through a bull market that's in its fifth year, with gains tougher to come by. Tuesday's trading was also volatile, with the S & P 500 and Nasdaq Composite dropping after rallying just the prior day. The broader index closed Tuesday just above 7,200.
