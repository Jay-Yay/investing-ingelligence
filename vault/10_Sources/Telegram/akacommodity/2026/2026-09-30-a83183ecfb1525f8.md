Russia Extends Diesel-Export Ban as Global Supply Squeezed
Russia extended a ban on most diesel exports through October, further tightening the global market just as demand rises ahead of the Northern Hemisphere winter and the US considers its own restrictions on exports.
Moscow enacted the ban on diesel exports for producers in July as a wave of Ukrainian strikes against its refineries dragged oil-processing rates to multiyear lows. The export curbs, initially envisaged to last just a matter of weeks, were subsequently extended through August and then to the end of September as the attacks continued.
