White Sugar Rally Adds to Already-Steep Refining Margins
White sugar futures in London rose for a third straight week, slightly outpacing raw sugar and keeping refining margins near their highest levels in two years.
A blistering rally, mirroring other crop markets at risk from El Niño, has both raw and white sugar up more than 10% so far this month. But prices for the processed sweetener rose more this week as raw supplies remain abundant in top producer and exporter Brazil, prolonging a trend this year of a widening premium for refined sugar over raw.
