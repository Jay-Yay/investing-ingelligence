US Oil Growth Faces Headwinds as Shale Producers Cut Spending
Some of the largest US oil companies are dialing back capital spending in the country’s shale basins as they use the windfall from high crude prices to boost shareholder returns and pay down debt rather than accelerate production growth.
Chevron Corp. and ConocoPhillips cut capital expenditure in the Lower 48 by 10% in the first six months of the year while Occidental Petroleum Corp. cut spending in the Permian Basin by 20% in that period, according to earnings reports. APA Corp., Matador Resources Co., and HighPeak Energy Inc. are also on track to spend considerably less on drilling and fracking in the US than a year ago.
