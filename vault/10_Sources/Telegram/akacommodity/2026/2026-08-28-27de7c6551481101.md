### Ukrainian Drone Attack Kills One, Sparks Fire at Yaroslavl Oil Refinery - The Moscow Times

(https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/08/28/ukrainian-drone-attack-kills-one-sparks-fire-at-yaroslavl-oil-refinery-a93595)

A Ukrainian drone attack killed one person and sparked a fire at one of Russia’s largest oil refineries in the city of Yaroslavl, Russian and Ukrainian authorities said Friday.
Yaroslavl region Governor Mikhail Yevrayev said one civilian was killed and 27 others were injured after falling drone debris hit an intercity bus.
He claimed that 67 Ukrainian drones were downed over the region, but that drone debris fell on residential areas, commercial properties and “industrial infrastructure.”
Ukraine’s military said it struck the Slavneft-Yanos refinery, which it said provides fuel and lubricants for the Russian military. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky later confirmed the attack and added that Ukraine’s SBU security service had struck a Tu-95MS missile-carrying aircraft in the Saratov region city of Engels.
Independent reports said that Ukrainian drones had already attacked Slavneft-Yanos at least seven times this year before Friday’s attack.
In the Moscow region town of Pavlovsky Posad, a drone slammed into a Magnit grocery store, blowing out windows at a neighboring clinic. Governor Andrei Vorobyov said the clinic remained open despite the damage.
Separate Ukrainian attacks in the southwestern Belgorod region killed two people and injured nine others over the last 24 hours, according to the local governor.
Russia’s Defense Ministry said it intercepted 512 Ukrainian drones across Russia and annexed Crimea between Thursday night and Friday morning.
