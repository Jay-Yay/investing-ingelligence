China Fund Closures Head for Eight-Year High as Demand Fades
China’s mutual funds are closing at the fastest pace in eight years as weak returns and investor redemptions leave hundreds of products too small to survive.
About 256 publicly offered funds have been liquidated this year, with another 46 warning investors they may soon close, according to Bloomberg-compiled data. That means potentially more than 300 products closing doors by year-end, the highest annual tally since a record in 2018, when sweeping industry reforms triggered shutdowns.
