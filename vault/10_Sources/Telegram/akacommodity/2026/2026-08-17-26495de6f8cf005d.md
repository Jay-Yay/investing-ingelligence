Greek-Run Oil Tanker Comes Under Attack in Russian Black Sea
A Greek-run oil tanker was attacked in the Black Sea after loading a Russian-origin cargo, the latest example of how the nation’s war with Ukraine is disrupting petroleum flows.
The Suezmax-class Skiros, able to hold about 1 million barrels of oil, was attacked after loading at the Caspian Pipeline Consortium’s terminal near Novorossiysk, people familiar with the matter said, asking not to be identified as the information isn’t public.
