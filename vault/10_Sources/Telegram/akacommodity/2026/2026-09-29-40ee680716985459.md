Chinese Stimulus Boost, Higher Bond Supply Threaten Debt Rally
China’s government bond supply is poised to pick up next quarter as the authorities look to speed up debt issuance to fund stimulus, potentially challenging the market’s rally.
The nation’s sales of new local government special bonds reached only 81% of the annual quota in the first nine months of the year, according to Bloomberg calculations. If the remaining quota is used by year-end, fourth-quarter issuance may exceed 820 billion yuan ($122 billion), the largest quarterly supply of such bonds in almost five years.
