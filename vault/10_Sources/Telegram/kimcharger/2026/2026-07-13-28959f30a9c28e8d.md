현지 미국인들이 미국 올리브영을 어떻게 느끼는지 엿볼 수 있는 생생한 후기 기사 공유드립니다.

여기서 느낀 점은

1) 여전히 미국 내 K-선크림에 대한 대기 수요는 폭발적. 미국 올리브영에서도 한국산 선크림을 구매할 수 있지 않을까 기대했던 소비자들이 많았음. 이번 자외선 차단 필터 승인에 대한 기대감도 엿볼 수 있음. (기사에서도 미국에서 K-뷰티를 알리는 데 K-선크림의 역할이 컸다고 언급.)

2) 세포라·아마존·얼타와 제품은 일부 겹치지만, 새로운 K-뷰티 브랜드를 구경하고 최신 뷰티 트렌드를 경험하기 위해 방문할 만한 요소가 충분. 특히 웰니스 코너는 색다른 재미 요소로 작용할 가능성이 있어 보임.

3) 아마존에서 제품을 접했지만, 실물을 보지 않고 구매하기에는 부담을 느끼는 소비자들의 잠재 수요도 존재.

4) K-헤어케어의 성공 가능성도 충분히 확인.

5) 미국 올리브영은 제품 진열 방식과 매장 구성 측면에서 기존 미국 리테일러(얼타·세포라)와는 확실한 차별점이 존재.


■올리브영의 미국 첫 매장은 최고의 K-뷰티 놀이터입니다.

두 시장에서 동일한 제품을 공급받고 계신가요?

꼭 그렇지는 않습니다. 한국 스킨케어 에 관심이 있으셨다면 , 전 세계적인 자외선 차단제 논란에 대해 잘 알고 계실 겁니다. 미국 식품의약국(FDA)은 자외선 차단제를 화장품이 아닌 의약품으로 분류하기 때문에 미국에서 새로운 자외선 차단 성분을 승인받는 데 수십 년이 걸립니다. 실제로 1999년 이후 처음으로 승인된 새로운 성분인 베모트리진올이 바로 이번 주에야 승인되었습니다 . 올리브영 미국 매장에서 판매하는 제품들은 FDA 규정을 준수해야 하므로, 온라인으로 주문하거나 매장에서 구매하는 자외선 차단제는 미국에서 아직 승인되지 않은 유명한 한국산 자외선 차단 성분을 제외하고 제조된 제품입니다.
이는 글로벌 웹사이트를 폐쇄하고 미국 매장 오픈과 동시에 미국 전용 웹사이트를 개설한 이후 고객들이 회사에 보낸 피드백의 핵심입니다. 많은 사람들이 입소문을 탄 선크림을 구매할 수 있을 거라는 기대감에 부풀어 있었습니다. (사실, 뷰티 오브 조선의 선 릴리프나 라운드랩의 버치 주스 모이스처라이징 선크림 같은 제품들이 소비자들에게 K뷰티의 매력을 알리는 데 큰 역할을 했기 때문입니다.)
CJ 올리브영의 글로벌 커뮤니케이션 리드인 레나 김은 제품 종류가 줄어든 이유에 대해 다음과 같이 설명했습니다. "한정판 제품, 콜라보레이션 제품, 프로모션 세트는 미국에서 판매하기 전에 추가적인 등록, 규정 준수 또는 온보딩 절차가 필요하기 때문입니다. 따라서 일부 제품은 나중에 출시될 수 있습니다." 브랜드 측에서도 인스타그램 게시물을 통해 이러한 상황이 주로 선크림 코너에 영향을 미친다고 언급했습니다. 하지만 여러 제품 리뉴얼에도 불구하고 올리브영 미국 매장의 선크림은 여전히 ​​훌륭했습니다. 파사데나 지점의 선크림 코너는 제가 일주일 전에 방문했던 세 곳의 올리브영 매장과 비슷한 규모였으며, 특히 서양 브랜드 제품이 상당히 많이 포함되어 있었습니다.

하지만 걱정하지 마세요. 김 대표는 미국 웹사이트에서 더욱 다양하고 폭넓은 제품 구성을 만나볼 수 있을 것이라고 밝혔습니다. 또한 새로운 자외선 차단 필터인 베모트리진올(BEMT)의 FDA 승인에 대해 묻자, 그녀는 이번 결정이 자외선 차단제 업계 전반의 혁신을 더욱 가속화할 것으로 기대하며, "소비자에게 도움이 되고 미국 미용 및 자외선 차단 시장의 지속적인 발전에 기여할 것"이라고 답했습니다. 이어 "이번 승인을 통해 가까운 시일 내에 더욱 발전된 자외선 차단제들이 시장에 출시될 수 있을 것으로 기대한다"고 덧붙였습니다.


매장 방문 경험이 너무 좋아서 이번 주말에 가족, 친구들과 함께 다시 갈 것 같아요(그리고 앞으로도 자주 갈 것 같네요). 이곳은 단순히 다른 곳에서는 구할 수 없는 제품을 찾는 곳이라기보다는 K-뷰티를 발견하는 즐거움을 주는 곳이라고 생각해요. 올리브영 미국 웹사이트처럼 제품 구성 자체는 특별히 독점적인 건 아니지만, 매장 안에서는 잘 모르는 브랜드를 구경하고, 식물 유래 카로틴, 화제의 PDRN , 시카 유래 성분인 마데카소사이드 같은 독특한 성분이 함유된 제품을 직접 만져보고 , K-뷰티 트렌드를 알아볼 수 있는 재미있는 경험을 할 수 있어요.
사실, 성수 올리브영에서 쇼핑하면서 가장 좋았던 점 중 하나는 파사데나 지점과 마찬가지로 건강 및 웰빙 코너가 있다는 것이었습니다. 콤부차, 건강 보조제, 차는 LA 사람들에게는 필수품이죠. 스낵 코너는 계산대 근처에 크고 밝은 초록색으로 꾸며져 있고, 산리오 굿즈도 판매하고 있습니다(사탕이나 과자 봉지 옆에 산리오 인형 키링을 놓을 공간이 있다면 말이죠). 쇼핑 리더로서 저는 사람들이 돈을 쓸 곳을 고를 때 놀라움과 즐거움이 얼마나 중요한 요소인지 잘 알고 있습니다.

올리브영 미국 매장은 울타뷰티, 세포라, 아마존 (인증된 판매자로부터 구매 가능)과 상당 부분 겹치는 부분이 많기 때문에, 뷰티 제품을 구매하면서 맛있는 간식도 함께 즐길 수 있다는 점이 사람들이 올리브영에 발걸음을 옮기는 이유가 될 수 있습니다. 길 건너 세포라나 차로 10분 거리에 있는 울타 뷰티 매장을 선택하는 이유 중 하나가 될 수 있겠죠. LA 동부의 빛나는 K-뷰티 명소인 올리브영을 다시 찾는 사람들을 끌어들이는 데는 이런 사소한 요소들이 중요할 겁니다. 그리고 저는 개인적으로 미니 사이즈 제품을 그냥 지나칠 수 없네요.

### I Shopped at Olive Young in Korea and the US. Here's the Biggest Difference  | Allure

(https://www.allure.com/story/olive-young-counter-service-shopping-experience)

Ever wonder what it’s like shopping with a beauty editor? Welcome to Counter Service, a series where we head into our favorite beauty boutiques IRL. We’re visiting the curated spaces and places that make shopping in person oh-so worth it—and you’re coming with. Step into America's very first Olive Young with us to shop all of K-beauty’s very best.
Exactly one week after I walked out of Olive Young’s flagship location in Seongsu, South Korea, I walked through the doors of the retailer’s first brick-and-mortar location in the United States in Pasadena, California. As Allure’s senior commerce director, comparing retail experiences is genuinely part of my job, so a few days home from my trip, I headed into the store, equal parts excited and jet-lagged.
The In-Store Experience
I came prepared to see throngs of people; after all, on opening day, customers camped overnight, and lines wrapped around the block. We arrived an hour before the store opened, and there were eager customers of all age ranges staking out their spots along the sidewalk.
Walking through the doors, you feel like you’re entering a pharmacy-Ulta Beauty hybrid. It’s brightly lit and welcoming; opposite the entry doors is a wall-to-wall screen flashing various Olive Young lettering treatments: puffy-paint pink, edgy chrome, and a reminder that Olive Young is an anagram for “All Live Young.” Off the bat, it felt extremely similar to the Olive Young stores I visited in South Korea, and so much of that comes down to the floor plan.
You’re immediately met by two “zones,” which are essentially pop-ups within the store dedicated to specific brands. The left is reserved for Mediheal, the Korean brand behind the viral Madecassoside Blemish toner pads, with a larger-than-life replica of the cyan box sitting next to the window. For scale, it’s about the size of those robot delivery creatures and, yes, the tweezer is included. The right zone is dedicated to Urban Decay, and while this may seem surprising to US customers, it’s not at all new to Korean shoppers. I saw plenty of global brands present at South Korean Olive Youngs. The same can be said for Olive Young’s US e-commerce site (which our K-beauty-obsessed editor reviewed in detail), where shoppers can find Kiehl’s, La Roche-Posay, Maybelline New York, Touchland, and plenty of Supergoop.
The assortment is generally organized by both brand and product type—something you’d expect in a big-box retailer or convenience store, but not necessarily from a specialty retailer. This layout is what characterized (and, I’ll admit, slightly overwhelmed) my experience shopping in the five-floor Seongsu Olive Young.
As an American, I’m used to getting swept up in storytelling through the retail experience, with brands moving you to purchase with emotion; by contrast, Olive Young is more straightforward. It orients you towards shopping by the desired result or ingredient, which can certainly make shopping easier for people who don’t know where to start. The masterminds at Olive Young know just where you want to begin your K-beauty journey: Sunscreen is positioned most prominently, as the first section you come across after the introduction zones, which brings me to the zinc oxide-coated elephant in the room.
Are You Getting the Same Products in Both Markets?
Not necessarily. If you’ve been keeping up with Korean skin care, you are probably aware of the global sunscreen discourse: The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) classifies sunscreens as drugs, not cosmetic products, which means it takes literal decades to approve new sunscreen filters in America—seriously, bemotrizinol, the first new filter since 1999, was approved just this week. The products stocked in Olive Young US must comply with FDA regulations, meaning the sunscreens you order online or pick up in-store have been reformulated without those famed Korean filters that are not yet approved in America.
This is the crux of the feedback customers have given the company since it closed its global site and launched a US-exclusive experience, coinciding with the store opening; much of the anticipation was the prospect of buying viral sunscreens. (After all, it’s products like Beauty of Joseon’s Sun Relief and Round Lab’s Birch Juice Moisturizing Sun Cream that helped consumers fall in love with K-beauty in the first place.)
Rena Kim, Global Communications Lead of CJ Olive Young, addressed this smaller assortment when I asked: “This is primarily because limited-edition products, collaboration items, and promotional sets require additional registration, compliance, or onboarding processes before they can be sold in the US. “As a result, certain products may become available at a later stage.” The brand also addressed this in an Instagram post, noting that this nuance primarily affects the sunscreen section. That said, even with the array of reformulations, I found that Olive Young US still shines when it comes to sunscreens. The Pasadena location’s SPF section rivals those at the three Olive Youngs I visited a week prior in size, albeit with an assortment that includes a hefty number of Western brands.
Don’t fret, though. Kim confirmed that customers can expect a broader and more diverse assortment on the US website. And when asked about the FDA approval of the new sunscreen filter, Bemotrizinol (BEMT), she said the decision is expected to further accelerate innovation across the category, “...benefiting consumers and contributing to the continued evolution of the US beauty and sun care market.” She added, “We also expect it will help bring more advanced sunscreen formulations to market in the near future.”
What I Added to My Shopping (IRL) Bag
Alternative Stereo Lip Potion Sugar Glaze Tint
The only real category I told myself I wouldn’t leave this Olive Young without was a new Korean lip tint. I prefer a slightly glossy finish as opposed to the more matte, blurred finish that characterizes most Korean tints, and I’m happy to report that I left with not one but two glossy Lip Potions, including this mini pot of the Alternative Stereo Sugar Glaze Tint in a made-for-summer coral. PS: You can get just a few shades of these on Amazon, and even the Olive Young US online shop does not offer the specific color I picked up.
Unove Frizz-Calming Glass Hair Oil (in Blush Musk)
I’ll take your glass skin and raise you a head of glass hair! The Korean hair-care brand Unove launched at Sephora earlier this year, and though I’ve tried and loved the leave-in, I haven’t been able to get my hands on the super-popular Glass Hair Oil. A little goes a long way to giving you super shiny and healthy-looking hair—even if it’s been heat-damaged to a dull oblivion like mine. I don’t like a strong hair scent in my products, so I could do with less musk, but it’s a small price to pay for the mirrorball effect.
Banila Co Romantic Blush Lip & Cheek (in #14 "Bouncing")
Yes, you can buy this product at Amazon or Ulta Beauty, but I couldn’t justify buying a “dupe” product for nearly $20 sight-unseen. I opted for a bright summer color, appropriately dubbed “Bouncing.” It’s super blendable, hasn’t broken out my temperamental skin, and Banila Co offers many more color options than Rhode, but it doesn't have as high a pigment payoff or staying power as the Pocket Blush.
Cell Fusion C Aquatica Cooling Sunscreen
I got five different sunscreens when I was in South Korea, but didn’t grab a Cell Fusion product, so this was my chance. I opted for the US version of the Cell Fusion C Aquatica Cooling Sunscreen, and I can see why it’s so well-loved. It absorbs instantly, feels weightless, and hydrates my skin—without making my eyes or skin sting.
Anua PDRN Hyaluronic Acid Hydrating Capsule Mist
There is so much to love about this face mist: the adorable packaging, the pump top that delivers an extremely even (and broad) mist, and, of course, the instant hydration. You can pair this with Anua’s “melting” collagen mask, but I plan to take this on the go. The top is very hard to get off the pod, but it’s not the worst thing in the world, since avoiding spills is ideal for this travel version of the larger mist.
What I Bought in South Korea
I went to three Olive Youngs in Seoul, and each was unique. The Seongsu flagship was full of tourists eager to buy sunscreen, and the entry floor was dedicated to a Pokémon cafe pop-up. I can’t help but shell out for merch, so I left with a few exclusive items, including limited-edition Snorlax and Squirtle makeup puffs. The smaller Olive Youngs I went to were more locals-oriented; they were much smaller and easier to navigate, and I could take some time to consider my purchases.
- Fromrier EGF Water Sun SPF 50: This was my favorite sunscreen that I picked up in Seoul. I’m sad to report that it is not available in-store in Pasadena. It’s a rare chemical sunscreen that doesn’t irritate my skin, and I bought a promo pack that included one full-size bottle and a mini keychain version that I carry everywhere.
- Jung Saem Mool Metal Serum Gloss: Glossy but not tacky, with a nice metal-tip applicator like Eadem’s Le Chouchou, and in colors that are so special, like a “mellowed vintage coral nude”. I found the makeup artist–founded brand in a part of the Seongsu store with higher-end products, dedicated gondolas, and more storytelling.
- Healingbird Revive No Wash Treatment: This is my favorite hair-care item from my overseas trip. It smells so good and leaves my hair noticeably softer. The packaging vibes particularly well with my Roz, Crown Affair, and Sidia countertop aesthetic, which is a plus.
- Naming Glowy Heart Lip Tint: Simply because of the super-cute packaging. I bought a handful to give out as gifts.
- Medicube Zero Pore Pad: This was sold out in-store at Olive Young in Pasadena—it’s no wonder, being one of the K-Beauty giant’s top-selling products. It’s so easy to use (just swipe and pat), and while I can’t say it’s zeroed out my pores just yet, it has helped mitigate the closed comedones on my forehead so far.
- Illiyoon Ceramide Ato Lotion: This is one you can buy in the States, but since my whole family uses it, we go through a lot of this product. Lovely, lightweight, and you can get every last drop; the formula is encased in an internal silicone bag—no pump-top dipping here!
What Surprised Me Most About Olive Young US
While much of the experience will feel natural—just more exciting and urgent, like shopping on the surface of a laptop screen with the brightness turned all the way up—there are a few things that made my eyes go wide.
- Individual product promotions are all around. The Olive Young team told me this is because, unlike seasonal sales popular in the States, individual brands determine when and how much they want to discount at any point in the year.
- There are experiential areas that are notably different from the traditional US experience. There are skin and scalp analyses, and a water basin where you can not only try out facial cleansers, but it also serves as an excellent general washing area after you’ve swatched every lip tint in the makeup section (it can’t just be me). “Rather than simply displaying products on shelves, we want to create an environment where customers can explore, learn, and build routines that work for them. That's a core part of the Olive Young experience,” says Kim. Since I was there before the full retail staff was clocked in, I was only able to test the skin analysis. It told me my skin wasn't as dry as I thought, but that oil was a bigger concern. The experience was quick, free, and helpful—or as helpful as a skin analysis could be when you’re wearing makeup.
- You’re starting from scratch with loyalty points. As someone who racked up a CVS-length receipt at Olive Young in Seoul, this is a bummer. With the e-commerce’s US migration, Olive Young Global members will need to start from scratch; your purchases won’t go as far, either, as Olive Young has instituted new thresholds for rewards tiers.
- The hair-care and body-care products are tightly curated (read: smaller than expected). The only reason I wasn’t shocked by just how petite these areas were (they’re about the same size as the color contacts section) was that I'd gotten that reaction out of the way during my overseas visit. The body care was relegated to about two vertical gondolas in Seongsu, and the hair care just three—though they did have hot tools plugged in and ready to go to test in front of a mirror, which is not something you get in Pasadena.
I’m excited to see how these two sections evolve, particularly hair care (we called it the next K-beauty wave in Starship Seoul, our dive into shopping Korean brands). Kim explained to me that haircare has evolved: “In South Korea, the hair-care market has evolved beyond basic cleansing and styling. Consumers increasingly view scalp care as the foundation of healthy hair, with scalp health, hair conditioning, and styling becoming part of a connected, long-term care routine.” Echoing this, another Olive Young employee told me that Korean brands are keen on preventative hair care, whereas she’s found that Americans tend to focus on reparative treatments. Kim explained, “The rise of 'scalp skinification,' treating the scalp with the same level of care as facial skin, combined with the slow-aging trend, is driving growth in specialized care products such as scalp ampoules, tonics, and exfoliating scalers.” She noted this is precisely why stores, including the Pasadena location, offer a dedicated scalp analysis service.
Final Thoughts
I loved the in-store experience and will probably go back with my family and friends this weekend (...and for many more weekends). I found it to be more of a K-beauty discovery engine rather than a ticket to products you truly cannot find anywhere else. Much like the Olive Young US site, the assortment itself isn’t particularly exclusive, but in-store? It’s a fun way to browse brands you may not be familiar with, get your hands on products with unique ingredients (like plant-derived carotene, buzzy PDRN, and madecassoside, a derivative of cica), and find out what’s trending in the K-beauty sphere.
In fact, one of my favorite things about shopping at Olive Young in Seongsu was the same as in the Pasadena location: the health and wellness section. What? Kombucha, supplements, and teas are table stakes for a certain type of LA-native. The snack zone has a large, bright-green footprint near the check-out area, and there is even some Sanrio merch you can pick up in the section (if you can fit the plushie keychains next to your candy and chip bags). As a shopping lead, I know that surprise-and-delight is a real factor when people consider where to spend their money.
