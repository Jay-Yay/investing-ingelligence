원문 (English Transcript)

John Collison:

"A lot of people are trying to put big models on the device. But the DRAM constraints right now are real, right?"

Sam Altman:

"Yeah. Look, putting a huge amount of DRAM in everyone’s phone or laptop is such a terrible use of global capital and manufacturing right now. Because what is the utilization rate of a phone? Like 5%? Maybe even less in terms of active model execution.

If you have a severe bottleneck on DRAM and memory manufacturing, you want that silicon running at 100% duty cycle in a data center serving thousands of people, not sitting idle in someone’s pocket 95% of the day.

Right now, the market is completely starved for compute and memory, and the 'invisible hand' is not clearing the market as quickly as you’d hope. Capital allocation into memory fabs has lagged behind where the actual demand curve is going, so every bit of memory we produce needs to be centralized where it gets maximum utilization."
