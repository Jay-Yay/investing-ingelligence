For decades, soft drinks have been a fixture of American life. While brands like Coca-Cola and Pepsi have long become household names across the globe, they remain widely recognized symbols of American consumerism. Among these regular consumers, 59 percent typically have a soda with their meals at home, while 47 percent drink them as refreshments in between. The category also travels well: roughly one-third enjoy soft drinks while eating out, looking for an energy boost, commuting or running errands.
