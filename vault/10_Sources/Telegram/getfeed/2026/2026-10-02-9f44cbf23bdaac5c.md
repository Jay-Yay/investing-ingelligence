Speaking of inflation, retail sales, which are expressed in nominal terms, grew 1.2 percent in August while consumer prices increased 0.4 percent. From a positive perspective, that means that Americans haven’t really cut back on their purchases despite the financial pain that inflation has inflicted on many households.
