According to Statista Consumer Insights, 63 percent of U.S. respondents listened to music via streaming in the 12 months preceding the survey, compared to just 11 percent who listened to music on vinyl records. At 24 percent, CDs are still surprisingly popular given their rapid decline in sales over the past two decades.
