AI가 (노동) 세계를 엄청난 속도로 재편하면서, 노동자들은 새로운 현실과 요구에 적응하거나 뒤처질 위험을 감수할 수밖에 없다. 48개국에서 약 5만 명의 노동자를 인터뷰한 PwC의 2026년 글로벌 인력 희망과 두려움 조사에 따르면, AI와 새로운 업무 방식을 받아들이는 사람들과 새로운 기술을 습득할 기회, 동기 또는 지원이 부족한 사람들 사이에 이미 격차가 벌어지고 있다. 조사에 따르면, 노동자의 64퍼센트가 지난 12개월 동안 직장에서 생성형 AI 도구를 사용했다고 답했으며, 이는 전년 대비 10퍼센트포인트 증가한 수치다. 5명 중 1명 이상은 이제 직장에서 매일 AI를 사용한다고까지 말했는데, 이는 AI 도구가 수백만 명의 근무 생활을 재편한 속도를 보여준다. 반대편에서는 응답자의 36퍼센트가 지난 한 해 동안 직장에서 AI 도구를 사용하지 않았고, 또 다른 19퍼센트는 AI를 한 번 또는 몇 번만 사용했다고 답했다. As AI is reshaping the (working) world at breakneck speed, workers have no choice but to adapt to the new realities and requirements or risk being left behind. PwC’s Global Workforce Hopes and Fears Survey 2026, for which almost 50,000 workers from 48 countries were interviewed, finds that a gap is already opening up between those embracing AI and new ways of working and those lacking opportunities, motivation or support to acquire new skills. According to the survey, 64 percent of workers have used generative AI tools at work in the past 12 months, up 10 percentage points from the previous year. More than one in five workers even said they use AI at work every day now, highlighting the speed at which AI tools have reshaped the working lives of millions of people. At the other end of the spectrum, 36 percent of respondents haven’t used AI tools at work in the past year, and another 19 percent said they only used AI once or a few times. According to PwC, it is this group of workers – the so called “engine room” cohort – that employers should pay attention to, as they represent the majority of the workforce, are responsible for most of the day-to-day work within an organization yet often feel replaceable and shut out of growth opportunities.The survey also reveals that adoption of AI agents, i.e. tools that can carry out complete tasks autonomously with or without human supervision, still lags behind the use of generative AI tools, with 48 percent of respondents saying they haven’t used an AI agent and two thirds reporting very limited use. Only 10 percent of workers reported daily use of AI agents so far, even though many experts see agentic AI as a key to unlocking AI’s full potential in the workplace. Agents enable workers to automate entire workflows rather than “just” providing answers to their promts.
