It's beginning to look a lot like Christmas – this applies to supermarket aisles already stocking huge amounts of holiday candy, but also to Americans' self-proclaimed shopping schedules. In a survey by Statista Consumer Insights, 44 percent of adults between the ages of 18 and 64 say they are starting their Christmas shopping before December.A further 19 percent say the are springing into action at the beginning of December, also presumably being very well-prepared come Christmas morning. Yet, there is also around a quarter of Americans that only start their gift shopping the week before Christmas or even later, with 4 percent beginning as late as Christmas eve. Another 8 percent say they don't buy gifts at all.Americans attitudes around Christmas mirror these responses (to some degree).
