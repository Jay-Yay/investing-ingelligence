### L’Oréal to Beiersdorf: How K-beauty is reshaping the innovation playbook for Western beauty brands

(https://cosmeticsbusiness.com/l-or%C3%A9al-to-beiersdorf-how-k-beauty-is-reshaping)

This article was originally published in the K-beauty Trend Report. Receive your copy here
K-beauty’s impact on the global beauty industry has been recognised for over a decade, but in 2026, a fascinating shift is taking place as its influence on Western beauty product innovation scales new heights.
L’Oréal Paris’ new Infallible Fresh Wear Cushion Foundation launch features prominently in its product communication that it is “inspired by K-beauty”.
Nivea Sun’s Silky UV Stick SPF50+ packaging features a ‘New from Korea’ label on the cap, with the Beiersdorf-owned brand citing that the product uses “state-of-the-art Korean stick technology.”
YSL Beauty provides another example. The marketing for the luxury brand's new Skin Affair Soft Glow Cushion Foundation says that it leverages “advanced Korean cushion technology”.
Meanwhile drugstore player Maybelline New York proudly references its viral Cloudtopia Cheek and Lip Mousse line as being “born in New York, made in Korea” with a whipped, airy consistency that replicates K-beauty’s blurred, soft-focus blush aesthetic.
Leading Western beauty brands such as these, and many more, are no longer just launching products that are inspired by K-beauty trends, they are directly referencing Korean innovation in their marketing.
