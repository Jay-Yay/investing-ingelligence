### AMD Notifies Partners of 10% Price Hike As Increased TSMC Wafer Costs Could Squeeze Ryzen CPU Lineup For Q4 2026

(https://buly.kr/3YGGx74)

Even though AMD didn't confirm price hikes for its Ryzen CPUs, the increased wafer costs could result in AMD CPUs becoming more expensive.
AMD Notifies its Partners About a 10% Price Hike for its Chips, Which Could Include its Ryzen CPU Lineup Due to Its Reliance on TSMC
It looks like it's time for another price hike, but this time it could also include CPUs. According to the latest report from ChannelGate, AMD could face another round of price increases later this year as rising manufacturing costs at TSMC put pressure on the company's chip business.
The report confirms that TSMC has notified its customers about the price hikes, leading to AMD notifying its partners about the same. The price hike is roughly 10% and is planned to be implemented in the fourth quarter of 2026. While this report specifically mentions AI accelerator chips, consumer graphics processors, and motherboard chipsets, it does not explicitly mention CPUs.
So, there is no confirmation of a price hike for Ryzen processors. However, the possibility of higher CPU prices should not be dismissed since AMD relies heavily on TSMC for its client CPUs. That means higher wafer costs at TSMC will directly affect consumer processor prices. It's AMD's general policy to pass the increased costs to its customers, and we could see higher Ryzen prices in Q4 2026.
Keep in mind that it's not just AMD that will be implementing a new price hike for its CPUs. Intel announced a similar hike for its client processors recently, as it now aims to focus more on products that bring higher profits. So, we aren't just looking at more expensive RAM, SSDs, and GPUs, but also more expensive CPUs in a month or two. Still, keep in mind that a 10% Ryzen price hike should be treated as a "possibility" rather than a confirmed development.
