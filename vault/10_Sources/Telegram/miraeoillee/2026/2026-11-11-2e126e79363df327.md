- SpaceX가 독일 태양광 장비업체 싱귤러스(Singulus)와 HJT 태양광 셀 생산라인 공급계약을 체결했습니다. 

- 첫 생산라인 계약금액은 6,000만 유로 이상이며, 납품은 2027년 상반기 예정입니다. 원화로는 약 960억원 이상입니다(유로당 1,600원 가정). 

- 지난해 싱귤러스 매출이 약 4,830만 유로였다는 점을 고려하면, 이번 첫 라인 계약만으로도 지난해 연간 매출을 넘어서는 상당히 큰 수주입니다.

- 생산능력이 얼마인지는 공개되지 않았으나, 1GW 미만일 것으로 추정됩니다. 향후 추가 생산라인 공급도 계획하고 있습니다만, 아직 확정된 바는 없습니다.

- 싱귤러스는 태양광 셀을 만드는 데 필요한 세정/박막 코팅 장비와 생산라인을 공급하는 독일 상장사입니다. 물론 2022년 HJT 턴키 제품을 출시하긴 했으나, 트랙레코드가 없어 이번 수주는 갑작스럽고 의아하긴 합니다.

- 최근 중국 태양광 장비에 대한 관세도 2개월 유예되어 Maxwell도 여전히 후보군이고, 비중국에서는 주성엔지니어링이 있기 때문입니다.

- 이는 머스크의 최근 행보와 연관지어보면 이해가 갑니다. SpaceX는 장비 발주에 앞서 싱귤러스에 전환사채 투자와 차입금 차환을 지원했습니다. 해당 CB가 모두 주식으로 전환되면 약 17%의 지분을 확보할 수 있는 구조입니다. 장비 구매를 넘어 제조 밸류체인에 대한 영향력까지 확보하려는 행보로 해석됩니다.

- 다만 아직까지는 싱귤러스의 기술력 및 양산기술이 확인되지 않은 상황이기에, 여러 장비 발주 후보를 두고 테스트를 진행하고 있다고 보는게 맞을 것 같습니다.

SINGULUS TECHNOLOGIES Announces Major Supply Contract with SpaceX for Advanced HJT Solar Cell Manufacturing Equipment
Kahl am Main, November 10, 2026
SINGULUS TECHNOLOGIES AG (“SINGULUS”), a global leader in the development of solar cell production equipment, today announced it has signed a supply contract with SpaceX for a turnkey production line for the manufacture of heterojunction solar cells. This supply contract is part of a strategic partnership with SpaceX under which SINGULUS is planning to deliver multiple additional lines.
As part of the partnership, SINGULUS will develop high-performance solar cells and production equipment based on proven technologies deployed at scale. The first production line will be delivered in the first half of 2027, and production will scale to support more than 100 GW of production equipment as SpaceX accelerates satellite manufacturing in Bastrop, Texas.
Lars Lieberwirth, Member of the Executive Board of SINGULUS, said: “Our unique platform approach, which combines wet chemistry, vacuum coating and thermal processing, is a strong differentiator in the market. The strategic partnership with SpaceX allows us to further industrialize these capabilities at a meaningful scale and solidify our position as a leading Western provider of next-generation solar cell technology.”
Mike Nicolls, SpaceX’s Senior Vice President of Starlink, commented: “Partnering with SINGULUS gives us a high-performance manufacturing path for solar cells for our Starmind satellites at our production facility in Texas. The combination of their process expertise and our long-term commitment creates a durable option for scaling solar production.”
SINGULUS entered a strategic partnership with SpaceX in March 2026. SpaceX provided significant funding to SINGULUS in March 2026 and additionally in October 2026 as part of the recently announced subordinated secured loan.
SINGULUS TECHNOLOGIES – Synergies in Vacuum Coating & Wet Processing
SINGULUS TECHNOLOGIES develops and assembles innovative machines and systems for efficient thin-film coating and surface treatment processes, which are used worldwide in the Photovoltaics, Semiconductor, Medical Technology, Packaging, Glass & Automotive as well as Battery & Hydrogen markets.
The company’s core competencies include various processes of coating technology (PVD sputtering, PECVD, evaporation), surface treatment as well as wet-chemical and thermal production processes.
SINGULUS TECHNOLOGIES sees sustainability as an opportunity to position itself with innovative products. In the focus are environmental awareness, efficient use of resources and avoidance of unnecessary CO₂ pollution.
SINGULUS TECHNOLOGIES attaches great importance to responsible and sustainable corporate governance.
