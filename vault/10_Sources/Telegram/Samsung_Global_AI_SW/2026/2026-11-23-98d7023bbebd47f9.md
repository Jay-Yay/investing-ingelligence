### Applied Digital Secures New 150 MW, 18-Year Lease with Tier-One Investment-Grade Hyperscaler, Bringing Contracted Base-Term Revenue Above $41 Billion :: Applied Digital Corporation (APLD)

(https://ir.applieddigital.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/165/applied-digital-secures-new-150-mw-18-year-lease-with)

Applied Digital Secures New 150 MW, 18-Year Lease with Tier-One Investment-Grade Hyperscaler, Bringing Contracted Base-Term Revenue Above $41 Billion
Total potential lease revenue nears $100 billion, with all renewal options
DALLAS, Nov. 23, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Applied Digital (NASDAQ: APLD), a U.S. based AI data center infrastructure company specializing in the design, development, ownership and operation of large-scale, purpose-built campuses serving hyperscale off-takers under long-term contracts, today announced that it has entered into a new 18-year lease agreement with a tier-one investment-grade hyperscaler for 150 MW of critical IT capacity.
The lease provides for approximately $5.4 billion in base-term contracted revenue, or approximately $12.7 billion if all renewal options are exercised over a 33-year total term. With this addition, the Company’s total contracted base-term lease revenue exceeds $41 billion, or approximately $100 billion if all renewal options are exercised.
The lease contains improved commercial terms over our prior leases, including higher pricing, as Applied Digital continues to scale its AI infrastructure platform. The 18-year duration gives the Company greater long-term revenue visibility.
With this latest lease, Applied Digital’s portfolio is over 1.6 GW of total contracted critical IT load and 2.2 GW of grid-connected peak load. Approximately 75% of the Company’s total contracted base-term lease revenue is now attributable to investment-grade, which we believe underscores the credit quality of its customer base and the strength of its long-term leasing model.
“Securing another large-scale, long-term lease with a tier-one investment-grade hyperscaler reinforces to us the strength of our customer relationships and the quality of our contracted revenue,” said Wes Cummins, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Applied Digital. “We believe our portfolio reflects the scale and sophistication of the infrastructure we’ve developed, and our focus remains on executing with the speed, discipline and consistency required for our customers.”
“We plan to finance this build through our established structure, including first-in equity already funded from our balance sheet and through our $5 billion programmatic equity partnership with Macquarie Asset Management, followed by project financing supported by the long-term lease with a tier-one investment-grade tenant,” said Saidal Mohmand, Applied Digital’s Chief Financial Officer. “We expect to complete the remaining project finance for Delta Forge 1 and Polaris Forge 3 in the first calendar quarter of 2027, followed by Delta Forge 2 in the second calendar quarter of 2027. This approach is designed to optimize our cost of capital while aligning capital deployment with construction timelines,” Mr. Mohmand continued.
Delivery of the capacity is currently anticipated to begin in the first half of calendar 2028, subject to applicable development milestones, utility arrangements and regulatory approvals.
About Applied Digital
Applied Digital (Nasdaq: APLD) is a U.S. based AI infrastructure company specializing in the design, development, ownership and operation of large-scale, purpose-built AI data center campuses.
Combining the fundamentals of technical real estate with the specialized requirements of digital infrastructure, the Company provides the mission-critical power, advanced liquid cooling and connectivity required to support artificial intelligence and high-performance computing workloads. Applied Digital’s AI Factory campuses are leased to hyperscale customers under long-term contracts, combining specialized infrastructure assets with predictable, long-term revenue streams.
Through its repeatable approach to design, development and operations, Applied Digital delivers highly engineered infrastructure designed to support the demanding requirements of AI computing at scale.
