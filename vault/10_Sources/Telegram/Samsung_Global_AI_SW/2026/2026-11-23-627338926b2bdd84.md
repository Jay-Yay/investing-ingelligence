: 특히 AI 에이전트가 실행되는 엔드포인트와 데이터에 접근하는 네트워크 연결을 동시에 통제. 양사의 보안 데이터를 통합해 AI 기반 위협 탐지 및 대응 역량도 강화할 계획

: 합병 기업의 ARR은 100억 달러 이상으로 확대될 전망. 2029년 기준 TAM은 1,850억 달러 이상 증가하며, 기존 CRWD 제품을 사용하지 않는 ZS 고객 약 8,000개를 대상으로 교차 판매 추진

Brings Zscaler’s Zero Trust SASE, Security for AI, and Data Protection to the Falcon Platform: Zscaler protects 50 percent of the Fortune 500 and operates the world’s largest distributed inline security cloud across more than 200 service locations. After closing,CrowdStrike will offer Zscaler’s Zero Trust SASE, security for AI, and data protection as part of the Falcon platform.
 
- 
Combines Device and Network Data: The Falcon platform generates trillions of events daily from the endpoint and cloud workloads, and Zscaler’s Zero Trust Exchange processes more than 750 billion transactions per day. After closing, device and network telemetry will converge into a single data set, allowing CrowdStrike’s SafeMind frontier security models to train on the adversary’s full attack path.
 
- 
Unlocks Cross-Sell Across Both Customer Bases: Approximately 8,000 Zscaler customers are not yetCrowdStrike customers. Within the shared customer base,CrowdStrike sees room for even more platform adoption. After closing, customers of either company will be able to add the other’s products on a single Falcon Flex contract. Both companies have best-in-class gross and net retention.
 
- 
Expands the Market and the Partner Ecosystem: CrowdStrike estimates the acquisition will expand its total addressable market by more than$185 billion in calendar year 2029. Zscaler’s solutions will be sold through CrowdStrike’s global system integrators, resellers, distributors, and cloud marketplaces, with significant opportunity downmarket and into the MSSP space.
 
- 
From Partnership to One Platform: The acquisition builds on one of CrowdStrike’s largest technology alliances, spanning more than 2,000 shared customers and 16 integrations. For years, customers have asked CrowdStrike andZscaler to join forces, and the two companies come together from positions of strength. A partnership integrates separate products. One company integrates everything: the data, the roadmap, the go-to-market, and the commercial model, and AI accelerates the integration. After closing, the integration ofZscaler into the Falcon platform will give customers one platform, one data layer, and one contract.
 
- 
Increases Scale and Accelerates Profitable Growth: At closing, the combined company is expected to begin with more than $10 billion in ARR.Zscaler reported$3.9 billion of ARR as of its recently completed fiscal quarter. The acquisition is expected to add incremental high-margin recurring revenue streams to CrowdStrike’s financial profile.
 
- 
Creates Significant Revenue Synergies: CrowdStrike expects revenue synergies to build progressively over the first 30 months following close, reaching approximately$1 billion in annualized run-rate revenue synergies by the end of that period. These revenue synergies are expected to come from deepening combined customer relationships, expanding cross-sell and upsell opportunities, and integrating Zscaler’s solutions into CrowdStrike’s Falcon Flex go-to-market motion.
 
- 
Drives Margin Expansion and Free Cash Flow Accretion: CrowdStrike expects to achieve$500 million in annualized run-rate cost synergies within 18 to 24 months following the close, primarily through optimization of its data center infrastructure, vendors, and broader operations. These initiatives will support additional operating leverage and margin expansion over time. The transaction is expected to be significantly and immediately accretive to free cash flow per share before factoring in any synergies. The transaction is also expected to be accretive to revenue growth, non-GAAP operating margin, and free cash flow margin, after synergies, within the first fiscal year after close.
