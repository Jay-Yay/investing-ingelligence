마이크로소프트, Ignite 2026 주요 발표 내용

: 마이크로소프트는 Ignite 2026에서 AI를 업무 전반에 통합하는 ‘프런티어 생태계(Frontier Ecosystem)’ 전략 발표. 사티아 나델라 CEO는 Copilot을 ’업무를 위한 새로운 운영체제(OS)’로 정의

■ Copilot 및 기업용 AI 플랫폼

: Copilot Workspaces 공개

→ 사용자와 여러 AI 에이전트가 공통의 업무 맥락을 공유하며 공동 목표를 수행하는 협업 환경. 24시간 자율적으로 업무를 수행하는 Autopilot과 맞춤형 앱 개발을 지원하는 Code도 소개

: Microsoft IQ 발표

→ Microsoft 365, Fabric, Foundry 및 외부 시스템의 데이터를 통합해 조직의 업무 맥락을 AI 에이전트에 제공. 수백 개의 신규 커넥터 추가 및 세일즈포스와 Copilot 통합 협력 발표

■ 자체 AI 모델 경쟁력 강화

: Microsoft-Decision-1.1 정식 출시

→ 멀티모달 지원과 추론 정확도 및 응답 속도 개선. 이미 4,000개 이상 고객사가 활용. GPT-6.2와 메타의 최신 모델도 Foundry에 도입

: MAI Code 2 및 Code 2 Flash 정식 출시

→ 복잡한 코딩 및 대량의 반복 작업을 각각 지원하며, GitHub Copilot과 Foundry에서 제공. 보안 특화 모델 Cyber 2 Flash도 공개. 신뢰할 수 있는 고객에게 자체 모델 가중치를 제공하는 MAI Licensed Weights 발표

■ AI 에이전트 관리 및 보안

: Agent 365 Business Analytics 발표

→ 기업의 AI 사용 비용을 실제 비즈니스 성과와 연결하고 ROI를 분석하는 관리 도구. Agent 365에 등록된 에이전트는 이미 5,000만 개 이상

: Microsoft Perception 정식 출시

→ 공격 시뮬레이션(레드팀), 공격 탐지(블루팀), 문제 수정 및 복구(그린팀)를 담당하는 AI 에이전트들이 협업하는 자율형 보안 시스템

: AI Transparency Initiative 발표

→ NIST 및 40개 이상 조직과 협력해 AI 시스템의 투명성, 독립적 검증 및 안전성에 관한 개방형 산업 표준 개발 추진

■ 데이터베이스 및 자체 인프라

: HorizonDB 정식 출시

→ Azure 기반 분산형 PostgreSQL 데이터베이스로, 자체 관리형 PostgreSQL 대비 최대 8배 성능 제공. Fabric과 통합해 실시간 트랜잭션 및 페타바이트 규모의 과거 데이터를 AI 에이전트가 함께 분석하도록 지원

## 첨부 기사 원문

### Ignite 2026: Building the frontier ecosystem - The Official Microsoft Blog

(https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/11/17/ignite-2026-building-the-frontier-ecosystem)

Satya Nadella, Microsoft Chairman and CEO, delivered the opening keynote at Ignite in San Francisco this morning. Here is an edited version of his remarks.
This week is about two things: First is how we capitalize on new capabilities that are growing exponentially. And second is how do we build the capability to deal with the consequences of these exponentials.
At times like these, I always find it helpful to be grounded in our mission: To empower every person and every organization on the planet to achieve more. In today’s world, that means building a frontier ecosystem. We need to parlay any technological advance into real outcomes and new points of leverage for people, organizations and society at large.
For us, that comes down to three things: Keeping human ambition at the center, ensuring your control of your organization’s intelligence and learning, and trust.
And so, our goal is to build a stack that addresses these three core architectural principles.
Copilot
Copilot is the new OS for work. Copilot brings together all the new form factors of human-computer interaction, whether it is Chat, Cowork, Code or Autopilot, into one experience. It also brings together the disparate categories of knowledge work, coding and business process. That is why we have all of Office with its richness built into Copilot. And underlying it is a model system that matches the right intelligence to the right context to the right form factor to accomplish the task.
Home gives you one place to start, whether you need a quick answer or want to assign and complete complex, multi-step tasks. Code brings the power of an infinite SaaS factory to every individual. It lets you build your own custom SaaS apps, dashboards and much more. And Autopilot is an autonomous agent that keeps moving your work forward 24×7 in all the places where you and your team collaborate.
Underlying all this is Work IQ. You can think of this as the most important database for any organization, with a deep understanding of people, their relationships, documents, emails, meetings, chats and projects that make up the fabric of work.
And we are going further. We are extending Copilot for specific roles: sales, legal, finance, service. We are organizing the skills, connectors and plugins for these roles across the entire surface area of Copilot.
And that includes third-party systems, too. In fact, today we are announcing new plugins for business applications, starting with Dynamics 365. And it is not just Dynamics. We are very excited to partner with Salesforce to bring Salesforce to Copilot.
And we are going beyond that as well. Up until now, we have mostly been in a single player world of people and their agent loops and swarms. But the time has come for a true multiplayer form factor, where people, their agents and other people are all driving towards a common goal, with shared context. This is Copilot Workspaces:
Microsoft IQ
The idea behind IQ is to help every organization build, retain and continuously improve its own intelligence. Essentially, we are taking the system we built underneath Copilot and externalizing it as a first-class platform that embodies your organizational intelligence as an asset that you continuously build and control.
We are bringing together all of the data you have across Fabric, Foundry and Microsoft 365. And, of course, all the third-party context too. Microsoft IQ comes with hundreds of new connectors we are announcing today, so agents can get the full enterprise context where business gets done. And all this rich context manifests as business plugins that can be shared and inherited across the organization.
Models
For us, it is about mapping intelligence to a given task, with the best economics. This is something we can uniquely do in the context of real-world tasks by tuning our products, harness and tools, and the model system, end-to-end.
And today we are thrilled to announce Microsoft-Decision-1.1, which is now generally available and ready for enterprise deployment. We have added multimodal support. And we have improved performance, making decisions more accurate and responses faster. Already, more than 4,000 customers have used it. And we ourselves are using it for core tasks like routing, data labeling, personalization, content filtering and hundreds of other use cases.
Foundry of course has all the most popular closed and open weight models, too. We are very excited to bring GPT 6.2 to Foundry. It is a great model that sets a new bar for AI in professional work. And today we are announcing that Meta’s latest models are all coming to Foundry as well.
We are also innovating at the frontier with our own MAI model family that is both compute-efficient when it comes to pre-training and token-efficient when it comes to product deployment. Our goal is to put additional points on the frontier curve informed by real world task-performance.
And we are going further. We are announcing MAI Licensed Weights, meaning our trusted customers will now be able to get access to Microsoft AI model weights through our Frontier Co.
Today, we are introducing lots of new models. But I want to highlight two in particular:
First, Code. We are introducing our new flagship family of agentic coding models: Code 2. Code 2 is at the frontier of cost-performance for everyday enterprise coding tasks. It excels in taking code to production, deep debugging, multifile implementation, repo-wide reasoning and UI at a fraction of frontier cost.
Code 2 Flash is designed for fast, high-volume work like code explanations, targeted edits, routine fixes and rapid iterations. It brings near-flagship quality for a much lower cost. These models are both generally available today on GitHub Copilot, both in the model picker and as part of Copilot Fusion. They are also available in Foundry.
We are taking the same approach in cyber. Cyber 2 Flash is our most capable security model yet. It is a flash-sized model that delivers performance ahead of frontier models at a fraction of the cost per task. And, just as we have done with coding, we are co-designing the model with the tools and systems where it will be used. We are integrating Cyber 2 with Perception.
Agent 365
Successfully deploying AI systems starts with making sure you have observability at every layer of the stack. That is where Agent 365 comes in. It is a control plane that extends your existing governance, identity, security and management systems to the agents in your enterprise. We already have over 50 million agents in Agent 365.
And it includes FinOps, too. As AI scales, every organization needs a clear line between what it spends and the value AI creates. That is why today we are announcing Agent 365 Business Analytics. You can think of it as your ERP system for AI usage. You can see exactly what is driving spend. You can connect spend to outcomes to drive business value. And you can optimize ROI across different scenarios.
Security
The next thing I want to talk about is how you take the environment you have set up, from Copilot to IQ to Agent 365, and secure it. And that is where Perception and our security stack comes in. We are building an end-to-end trust platform. We are helping customers both secure their AI deployments and use AI to strengthen their security posture.
Perception is a complete agentic security system with teams of red, blue and green agents that run through playbooks to deliver security outcomes at machine speed, with you always in control.
Essentially, think about it like this: red team agents simulate attacks, blue team agents detect attacks and green team agents fix and remediate. With Playbooks, you can compose teams of agents to tackle complex jobs and trigger them to take action autonomously.
Perception helps connect all the signals, sensors and actuators across your identity, client, cloud, apps and agents to intelligence to secure your end-to-end environments. And I am very excited to share that Perception is now generally available.
In addition to the work we have been doing across our products, we are also working across the ecosystem to ensure everyone can innovate at speed and scale, safely. Today, we can see almost everything models touch. But we cannot see what happens inside the model. And what the model tells us about itself is not independent evidence. These nested black boxes are simply unacceptable. We need to be able to examine what AI sees and does, verify its actions independently and control what it is allowed to do.
That is why today we are also announcing the Microsoft AI Transparency Initiative.
There are three parts to this: First, we will expand our collaboration in NIST’s Agent Standards Initiative, drawing on its research to help lead the development of open, industry-wide standards for AI transparency, security and governance.
Second, we are taking a full ecosystem approach. Together, we can enable AI systems that are accountable, independently verifiable and safe by design, regardless of the model or provider. Already, 40 organizations are working with us on defining and applying these standards.
Third, we are working to incorporate the same standards in our products, including MAI models, Foundry and Copilot. Our ambition through our research, our product work and our ecosystem is to help establish an open industry standard that makes AI safer, more transparent and more accessible.
Cobalt 200 VMs
Underneath all of this is unbelievable silicon. We are building a heterogeneous platform working across the ecosystem to bring you all the latest innovation. We are also advancing our own hardware systems, co-designing silicon, servers and infrastructure to get more from every watt, chip and datacenter.
We just announced Maia 300, which will deliver 40 percent more tokens per watt compared with leading GPU-based systems. Azure Boost and Azure Integrated HSM move critical infrastructure and security functions into purpose-built silicon. And today we are announcing general availability of our next-generation Cobalt 200 VMs, which are our highest-performing general-purpose VMs with leading price-performance.
So, that is a quick walk through some of our news. When you look at all that is coming, it is clear that we are entering an era of ubiquitous intelligence. The key imperatives for us are to ensure human ambition, a firm’s control of its own intelligence and the diffusion of the benefits broadly. That is the promise of a frontier ecosystem. And that is what we are here to build together.
For more information on today’s announcements, check out the Book of News.
