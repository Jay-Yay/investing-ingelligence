### Design the Muse App, Just for You

(https://about.fb.com/news/2026/11/introducing-spaces-customize-muse-app/)

Today, we’re introducing spaces, an interactive way to build a more personalized Muse. A space is a custom experience you create inside Muse by describing your idea, like a holiday gift tracker, a daily word game, or a planning board for your next DIY project. It’s shaped around your routines, goals, and interests, and can be as practical or entertaining as you want. Every space you create becomes part of Muse, so you can keep it where you want and come back to it any time.
From An Idea to Something Real
Have an idea you’ve been meaning to try? Tell Muse about it, and it builds it for you. Your initial idea is just the starting point. As you use your space, you can keep adjusting it with Muse, whether that’s adding what’s missing, trying a new look, or changing how it works.
You can also choose where your spaces live in Muse. Add them to your sidebar, move them into your main tabs, hide the ones you don’t need right now, and schedule when they appear. For example, keep your weekly planner up front during the workweek, then swap it for your weekend plans on Saturday.
What You Can Build
Spaces can help you get things done, have a little fun, or give your creative projects a home. Here are a few ideas:
- A shopping hub that brings holiday and birthday gift ideas, wishlists from Instagram saves, and your Marketplace listings and buyer messages into one view
- A school dashboard that finds the events relevant to your kids in school emails and adds them to your calendar
- A music tracker for the artists you love, with new releases and upcoming shows
- A wedding planner with your RSVP list, vendor invoices, and to-dos
- A trivia game that quizzes you on your favorite movies, music, or sports team
- A podcast studio to keep your episode ideas, research, and scripts together
- An order tracker for your small business, with new orders, customer messages and what needs to ship today
- A client hub for your freelance work, with projects, deadlines, and invoices
- A trip planner with your flights, hotel, reservations and a day-by-day itinerary.
Got a half-finished idea sitting in your notes app, a spreadsheet you keep meaning to clean up, or a hobby you want to dive into? Chances are you can turn it into a space.
A Space That Keeps Up With You
Your spaces are personal from the start. Connect the apps you use, and your spaces keep themselves up to date in the background, so new concert dates, price changes, or savings progress show up without you having to ask. You can check things off, add notes, and make edits right in your space, and everything will be there when you return.
Looking Ahead
Starting today, spaces are available everywhere Muse is available. We’re also exploring ways for you to share your spaces with others to build a more collaborative experience in Muse.
We’re also rolling out specialized versions of our models to Muse, trained with learnings from Muse Wonder, to make your overall experience even better.
