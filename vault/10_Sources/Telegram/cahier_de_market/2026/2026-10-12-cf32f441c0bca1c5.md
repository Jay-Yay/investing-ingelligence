Microsoft, Azure Maia 시스템을 멀티 GW 규모로 확대

1. Maia 200 상용화 및 Maia 300 출시
Microsoft는 2027년부터 Azure Maia 제품군을 멀티 기가와트(GW) 규모로 확대 배치하고, 자체 제품과 서비스의 주력 AI 추론 시스템으로 활용할 계획입니다.

현재 Maia 200은 Microsoft Copilot을 비롯한 상용 서비스에 적용되고 있으며, OpenAI 모델, 자체 MAI 모델, 주요 오픈소스 모델을 구동하고 있습니다. GitHub Copilot과 Microsoft Foundry도 연말까지 도입할 예정입니다.

미국·유럽·호주에 배치 중인 Maia 200은 주요 GPU 인프라 대비 와트당 토큰 처리량을 최대 1.7배 향상시키고 있습니다.

2. Maia 300 성능 및 특징

Maia 300은 롱컨텍스트 추론, 메모리 집약적 작업, 디코딩 중심의 AI 추론, 다단계 에이전틱 AI에 최적화된 3세대 자체 가속기입니다.

시스템 전반의 효율성 개선을 통해 주요 GPU 기반 시스템 대비 메가와트당 토큰 처리량이 40% 높을 것으로 예상됩니다. 또한 96개 가속기로 구성된 랙에서는 Maia 200 대비 최대 7배의 토큰 처리량을 제공합니다.
핵심 기술은 다음과 같습니다.

(1) 데이터 이동 최소화: AI 추론의 주요 병목이 연산 능력보다 데이터 이동 효율로 변화하고 있습니다. 소프트웨어 정의 데이터플로와 AI 기반 커널 최적화로 메모리 접근 및 데이터 이동량을 줄입니다.

(2) HBM4 대역폭 확대: HBM4 8스택을 탑재해 18TB/s의 메모리 대역폭과 950W TDP를 구현합니다. 온칩 SRAM을 스크래치패드 메모리로 활용해 HBM 접근 부담을 낮춥니다.

(3) 칩렛 아키텍처: 연산, 메모리 컨트롤러, I/O 기능을 분리해 효율성과 확장성을 개선합니다. 연산 칩렛은 TSMC N2P 공정을 사용합니다.

(4) KV Cache 최적화: 개선된 MXFP4 등 저정밀도 데이터 형식을 활용해 연산 및 KV Cache 저장에 필요한 메모리 용량과 대역폭을 줄입니다. 이를 통해 더 긴 컨텍스트와 높은 처리량을 지원합니다.

(5) Scale-up 네트워킹: 이더넷 기반 맞춤형 AI 통신 기술을 적용합니다. 가속기 간 고대역폭 연결을 텐서 병렬화에 최적화하고, 단일 랙부터 데이터센터 규모까지 확장할 수 있도록 설계했습니다.

3. Maia 300 핵심 사양

(1) 출시 예정: 2027년

(2) 반도체 공정: TSMC N2P

(3) HBM: HBM4 8스택, 가속기당 약 288GB 용량 및 18TB/s 대역폭

(4) 가속기 전력: TDP 950W

(5) 랙 구성: 가속기 96개, 총 HBM 용량 27.6TB, 총 HBM 대역폭 1.7PB/s

(6) 랙 전력: 167kW

(7) GPU 대비 효율: 주요 GPU 기반 시스템 대비 MW당 토큰 처리량 40% 향상 예상

(8) Maia 200 대비 성능: 랙당 토큰 처리량 최대 7배 향상

Microsoft는 이러한 시스템 집적도와 메모리 대역폭 확대를 통해 랙당 동시 AI 처리량을 높이고, 대규모 AI 서비스 운영의 전력 효율과 비용 경쟁력을 개선할 계획입니다.

### Microsoft to expand Azure Maia system to multi-gigawatt deployments - The Official Microsoft Blog

(https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/10/12/microsoft-to-expand-azure-maia-system-to-multi-gigawatt-deployments/)

Azure Maia 200 now in production; Azure Maia 300 coming in 2027
As we move into the era of Super Intelligence, Microsoft is entering a new phase of infrastructure. We will scale our Azure Maia family to multi-gigawatt deployments starting in 2027, making Maia the primary inference system for Microsoft products and services.
The multigenerational Azure Maia program has become a foundational element of Microsoft’s AI infrastructure strategy. Maia 200 is now serving Microsoft production workloads, including Microsoft Copilot, and supporting the MAI team as they develop next-generation models, with GitHub Copilot and Microsoft Foundry to follow by end of year. Across these workloads, Maia 200 runs OpenAI models, Microsoft’s own MAI models and leading open-source model families. Insights from the scale of Microsoft’s AI services allow for unique co-design between hardware systems and software applications to optimize real-world use cases across enterprise applications and industry scenarios.
Maia 300 is a complete intelligent system designed for the new types of AI workloads emerging today: Long-context reasoning, latency-sensitive conversational experiences and sustained, multi-step agentic tasks. By improving efficiency across the system, we expect Maia 300 to deliver 40% more tokens per megawatt compared to leading GPU-based systems. And in a full 96-accelerator configuration, it delivers up to 7x the rack-scale token throughput of Maia 200.
Maia 200: Driving the workloads that Microsoft runs at scale
The Azure Maia system is designed around the real-world workloads Microsoft runs at scale and the models we expect to build in the years ahead. Because silicon development takes years, our software and hardware teams collaborate early to anticipate architectures, scale and capabilities, translating those requirements into greater memory capacity, higher bandwidth and more accelerators operating as a single system.
That co-engineering is already delivering measurable efficiency gains. Maia 200 is deploying across the United States, Europe and Australia and delivering up to 1.7x more tokens per watt for built-in AI capabilities in Microsoft Teams, Copilot and Cowork experiences compared with leading GPU infrastructure.
Experience running our AI models and services on Maia 200 directly informed the design of Maia 300. Together, the two generations are designed to support a broad set of additional Microsoft’s AI services: multi-step agentic work that will help GitHub deliver coding experiences more efficiently; cloud-scale model serving that will help Microsoft Foundry support diverse architectures and deployment environments; inference and data generation for the MAI team; and latency-sensitive interactions that will help Dragon Copilot scale workflows and reduce clinicians’ administrative burden.
Maia 300: Scaling more useful AI within every megawatt
AI is becoming a foundational layer of every application, workflow and business process. As enterprises move beyond chat experiences to systems of agents that can reason, act and collaborate, AI infrastructure must deliver intelligence securely and efficiently at scale while continuously adapting as models and workloads evolve.
As the third generation of our Azure Maia program, Maia 300 is designed with an inference-first architecture optimized for long-context, memory-intensive and decode-heavy AI workloads. This involves balancing compute, memory bandwidth, networking and power efficiency at cloud scale.
Key capabilities of the Maia 300 system include:
Less data movement, more useful compute: Moving data efficiently, rather than raw compute power, is increasingly the bottleneck in large inference systems. Maia 300’s software-defined dataflow architecture and AI-assisted kernel optimization work together to maximize locality, reduce data movement and improve utilization across compute, memory and networking resources. By treating dataflow as a first-class design principle, Maia 300 delivers a significant performance-per-watt advantage over traditional GPU architectures.
Higher memory bandwidth within every watt: As AI workloads become increasingly context and memory-bound, Maia 300 delivers industry-leading HBM bandwidth per watt. Eight HBM4 stacks provide 18.0 TB/s of peak memory bandwidth within a 950-watt TDP, enabling efficient execution of large models and long-context workloads. Specialized kernel support uses on-die SRAM as a software-managed scratchpad, reducing pressure on HBM.
Chiplet architecture for enhanced efficiency: A new multi-chiplet architecture separates compute, memory-controller and I/O functions, improving efficiency, scalability and cost structure. Compute chiplets are built on TSMC’s N2P process node to maximize performance-per-watt.
Larger context, richer intelligence: By designing for advanced low-precision formats, including enhanced MXFP4, Maia 300 reduces memory and bandwidth requirements for both computation and KV-cache storage. The result is larger context windows, higher throughput and improved model efficiency within the same power envelope.
Scale-up networking at datacenter scale: Maia 300 treats networking as a seamless extension of the software-defined dataflow architecture. Rather than moving data through separate communication layers, dataflow operations span compute, memory and network resources as a single system. High-bandwidth interconnects between Maia accelerators are optimized for tensor parallelism, while a custom AI transport delivers performant and reliable scale-up communication across accelerators and racks. The AI-optimized transport leverages Ethernet-based networking infrastructure to enable a unified topology that scales efficiently from a single rack to datacenter-scale deployments.
Higher AI throughput from every rack: Maia 300 rack systems bring together 96 accelerators within a 167-kilowatt power envelope, delivering 27.6 TB of HBM capacity and 1.7 PB/s of aggregate HBM bandwidth across a high-speed scale-up domain. That density allows Microsoft to serve more simultaneous AI interactions and larger workloads from each rack, improving datacenter capacity and the economics of expanding AI services.
Maia 300 will begin production deployments in 2027. For more information on Maia 300 architectural details, read our technical paper.
Azure Maia is at the heart of our Frontier Transformation
The future will not be defined by a single model or a single piece of silicon or a single innovation. Model and silicon choice are inherent in our vision for Frontier Transformation. Our focus is on developing responsible, affordable and scalable platforms that will help everyone amplify their intelligence to drive breakthrough outcomes that matter for them.
Microsoft is unique because we are able to optimize from silicon to systems to agent on an open, heterogenous and model diverse platform our customers can count on for their AI transformation, today and in the years ahead. This platform is at the heart of Frontier Transformation, and Azure Maia is a foundational component of our approach to expand access, accelerate innovation and help customers capture more value from AI.
Next month at Microsoft Ignite 2026 in San Francisco and online we’re looking forward to sharing more information on our strategy, technology adoption trends and success in Frontier Transformation.
For more details, photos and videos, visit our Maia 300 site.
