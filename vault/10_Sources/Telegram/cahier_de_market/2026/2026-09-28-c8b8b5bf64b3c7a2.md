(핵심부분 발췌)

- 최근 우리는 공간 지능(spatial intelligence)의 핵심 난제 중 하나인 새로운 카메라 시점 예측(new camera view prediction) 문제를 해결하는 최초의 옴니(omni) 모델 아키텍처인 Atlas를 공개했습니다.

- LLM이 주어진 텍스트로부터 다음 토큰을 예측하듯이, Atlas는 처음부터 새롭게 학습되어 2D 이미지 입력으로부터 다음 시점을 예측할 수 있습니다. 또한 특정 과제에 특화된 전문 모델들보다도 뛰어난 성능을 보이며 현재 최고 수준의 결과를 넘어섰습니다.

- Atlas는 생성형 모델과 다중 시점 기하학(multiview geometry)을 결합함으로써, 컴퓨터 비전 분야의 오랜 난제였던 희소 재구성(sparse reconstruction) 문제를 사실상 해결했습니다.

- 이 기술은 매우 직접적이고 광범위한 파급효과를 가질 수 있습니다. 디자인과 엔지니어링부터 과학, 로보틱스에 이르기까지 다양한 분야에 영향을 미칠 수 있습니다.

- World Labs에서 우리는 매우 짧은 시간 안에 상당한 연구 성과와 기술적 돌파구를 만들어냈고, 이는 우리의 야망을 더욱 키웠으며 AI가 공간과 물리적 세계의 문제를 해결할 수 있는 잠재력에 대해 더욱 명확한 비전을 갖게 해주었습니다.

- 이제 그 가능성이 실제로 존재한다는 구체적인 증거를 확보한 만큼, 우리는 그 미래를 앞당기기 위해 할 수 있는 모든 것을 하고자 합니다.

- 이를 위해서는 우리의 노력을 더 큰 규모로 확장하고, 영향력을 넓히며, 하드웨어에 더욱 가까이 다가가야 합니다. 집중적인 하드웨어 개발 노력 없이는 AI의 효율성이 제약받습니다.
확장성에도 한계가 생깁니다.
그리고 우리가 추구하는 목표의 관점에서는, AI가 여전히 디지털 세계 안에 갇혀 있게 됩니다.

- 이것이 우리가 AMD에 합류한 이유입니다.

## 첨부 기사 원문

### To Seek a Newer World  - Dr. Fei-Fei Li

(https://drfeifei.substack.com/p/worldlabs-joining-amd?utm_campaign=posts-open-in-app&triedRedirect=true)

“Come, my friends, ‘Tis not too late to seek a newer world.”
– Lord Tennyson, Ulysses
World Labs is joining AMD. This is a huge moment for World Labs, our team, and for me, and I wanted to take a moment to share what this means and why I’m so excited for this next chapter.
A coming of age story
AI to me is both personal and civilizational.
I entered the field as a fresh graduate student in the year 2000, chasing my curiosity for solving the problem of visual intelligence for machines and the brain. I didn’t know it then, but these were the first steps of a career that’s been (to my amazement) at the center of AI’s coming of age. The early work from my lab called ImageNet helped usher in the age of modern AI, along with neural network algorithms and their implementation on GPUs. Since then, I’ve spent my entire career pushing AI from largely an academic curiosity to become the most innovative and transformative technology of our era.
Throughout this time, from my research in computer vision, deep learning, robotics and healthcare, to founding Google Cloud’s AI unit as a chief scientist, to becoming the founding director of Stanford’s Human-Centered AI institute, my work has always been guided by the desire to solve real world problems, hand in hand with humans for societal betterment. When generative AI began its transformation, and frontier AI research at scale moved from academia to industry, I knew the path with the deepest and broadest potential for positive impact would be creating a startup to tackle the most audacious technical problems.
Together with my incredible co-founders Ben Mildenhall and Justin Johnson, two world class AI researchers who have each moved the field in significant ways, I founded World Labs at the beginning of 2024. Our goal was to build a frontier lab focusing on some of the most important challenges in AI, which if solved, would dramatically improve AI’s ability to tackle real world problems.
The world is not just made up of words
We built the company guided by the foundational belief that language alone isn’t sufficient for model development. The universe isn’t made up of words; it’s made of real things. So many of the most important problems for AI to solve, from science to entertainment to robotics, require models to reason over the structure and behavior of the physical world. 
Tackling this problem domain requires a unique team with wide ranging skills including data, computer vision, large model training, systems and even computer graphics. I feel very lucky to have built a founding team of researchers and engineers that contains world experts across these areas. Together, we set out to train new classes of foundation models to tackle the hardest problems in spatial intelligence, and we’ve made significant strides toward our goals. 
Since our founding in 2024, World Labs has built leading AI spatial intelligence capabilities for everything from creative work to design. We built the world leading model training team for images, video and spatial reconstruction. And, with the acquisition of SceniX, we’re building towards an industry leading capability for robotics simulation.
Recently we released Atlas, a first of its kind omni model architecture that solves a key outstanding problem in spatial intelligence: new camera view prediction. Like LLMs can predict the next token from a line of text, Atlas, trained from scratch, can predict the next view from an input of 2D images, outperforming state of the art results even by specialized models. It has essentially solved a long standing problem in computer vision called sparse reconstruction, by combining generative models with multiview geometry. This has direct and far reaching consequences: from design and engineering to science and robotics.
We’ve seen incredible interest in Atlas across many domains: RL environments for robotics; scene generation for therapy and entertainment; and real world reconstruction for real estate, design and construction, and so much more to come.
Following our North Star to AMD
The research and technical breakthroughs we achieved at World Labs in such a short amount of time have only fueled our ambition and given us a clear vision for the potential for AI to solve spatial and physical world problems. Now that we have tangible proof of the possibilities, we want to do everything we can to accelerate the future.
To do this requires scaling our efforts, widening our reach, and getting closer to the hardware. Without having a focused hardware effort, AI is hobbled in efficiency. And scale. And for our purposes, remains trapped in the digital world.
Which brings me to AMD. We’ve partnered with AMD since our earliest days. AMD’s CEO, Dr. Lisa Su, was an early investor and believer in our mission, and we shared a belief that AI could propel society toward a better future. She has also been a great friend to me as we each journey through the world of technology.
We began a deep technical partnership last year starting with model training and inference optimization on AMD GPUs. We’ve seen up close how Lisa is boldly leading AMD as a major player in the hardware ecosystem with incredible momentum. As our teams worked together, we realized what a natural fit it would be to bring together our AI ecosystem of software and hardware, foundation models and applications.
Just like I knew it was time to do a startup to jumpstart the work of creating foundation models beyond LLMs and achieve the most impact in AI, it’s again very clear that the right path for myself and World Labs is to continue our mission as a part of AMD. Together we’re committed to keep pushing the frontier of AI to solve real problems, and doing so openly with the community and the entire ecosystem. We’re unwavering in our mission, and even more excited to continue building a world leading frontier research organization that is durable for the decades ahead. And we are committed to providing the best open models and platforms for running AI end to end from hardware to software to models to the data platforms needed to fuel it all.
Solving real problems, for human betterment
We’re extremely thrilled to be part of a shared journey towards the future we want to build together; where powerful AI platforms and systems are developed and deployed to help solve the most fundamental problems that humanity faces: from drug discovery, to unleashing human creativity; from transforming learning to unlocking the next generation of robotics.
And I can’t be more excited to join as an Executive Vice President and Chief Scientist, working directly with Lisa and her team. My career has led up to this moment - to continue growing and running a frontier research team, within a leading AI company, as a scientist and a technology leader. With this expanded platform and opportunity, I feel a deep sense of humility, gratitude and hopefulness that we will build a future we believe in, guided by the same North Star that has always inspired me to build technology in service of humanity. I am beyond excited for the future and grateful for the opportunity for World Labs and AMD to build it together.
