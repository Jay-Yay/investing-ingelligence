[News] NVIDIA Reportedly Suspends AI Server Shipments to Four Southeast Asian Countries amid Tighter U.S. Curbs
Tightening U.S. export controls are reportedly disrupting NVIDIA’s AI server shipments to Southeast Asia. According to Financial Times, NVIDIA has instructed its manufacturing partners to halt server deliveries to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Indonesia as Washington seeks to restrict Chinese access to advanced AI computing power. Partners including Dell and Supermicro have reportedly informed affected customers that NVIDIA will not authorize their shipments. It remains unclear whether the suspension is temporary while NVIDIA and its partners work to comply with the new rules, the report adds.
The four countries have emerged as popular destinations for Chinese AI developers seeking access to NVIDIA’s most advanced chips, which are subject to U.S. export restrictions. Many Chinese companies have circumvented these controls by renting computing power from cloud service providers operating servers equipped with NVIDIA chips. To close this loophole, the U.S. Department of Commerce introduced new restrictions last week, giving cloud providers 90 days to obtain licenses by demonstrating that their end users do not include restricted entities, the report notes.
Southeast Asia represents a significant business hub for NVIDIA. The report, citing company accounts, notes that the chipmaker recorded US$104 billion in revenue through its Singapore subsidiary in 2025, up 72% year-on-year. NVIDIA also books a portion of its revenue from Taiwan and China through the Singapore entity, the report adds.
The new U.S. restrictions also raise uncertainty over Malaysia’s data center expansion. According to The Straits Times, Malaysia approved MYR 123.5 billion in data center and cloud computing investments in the first nine months of 2026, accounting for 35.2% of its total approved investment, based on data from the Malaysian Investment Development Authority (MIDA).
U.S. Curbs Disrupt GPU Sales and AI Infrastructure Financing
In another sign of the impact of tightening U.S. export controls, sellers are reportedly seeking new buyers for thousands of NVIDIA B300 GPUs. According to CNBC, citing sources, an unusually large volume of NVIDIA GPUs has been put up for sale since the new measures were announced on November 16. The chips were reportedly intended for data centers where Chinese companies would rent computing power.
The disruption also extends to AI infrastructure financing in Southeast Asia. Bloomberg revealed that lenders had suspended work on a US$480 million loan sought by Singapore-based neocloud provider Aolani Cloud to finance NVIDIA GPU purchases for a data center in Malaysia, where ByteDance was set to remotely access computing power. Aolani said it is working with regulators and financing partners to ensure the project complies with the new rules.

### [News] Huawei May Unveil Custom HBM Architecture with 2x HBM4 Interface Width and Hybrid Bonding

(https://vo.la/BOzKIp6)

[News] Huawei May Unveil Custom HBM Architecture with 2x HBM4 Interface Width and Hybrid Bonding
Huawei is set to offer a closer look at its self-developed high-bandwidth memory (HBM) architecture. According to STAR Market Daily, citing the preliminary ISSCC 2027 agenda, Huawei will present a custom HBM design featuring 64 channels, a 4096-bit interface, twice the 2048-bit width of the HBM4 standard, and five-layer wafer-to-wafer (W2W) hybrid bonding. The design also integrates memory controllers with stacked memory, highlighting Huawei’s efforts in 3D integration, ITHome notes.
Huawei has already incorporated self-developed HBM into its Ascend 950 series, with the 950PR offering 128GB of memory and 1.6TB/s of bandwidth, and the 950DT featuring 144GB and 4TB/s, STAR Market Daily notes.
As EE Times China notes, the industry is exploring a shift to 4096 I/Os in future HBM generations, which would significantly narrow interconnect pitches and make hybrid bonding essential. However, Samsung Electronics and SK hynix have both delayed its adoption due to relaxed thickness requirements, with the technology expected to debut in 16-layer HBM4E at the earliest, the report adds.
Providing further details on Huawei’s approach, Sisa Journal notes that its W2W hybrid bonding technology involves bonding entire wafers rather than individual dies. While this enables vertical interconnection across multiple circuit layers, it presents significant manufacturing challenges due to stringent requirements for wafer alignment accuracy and uniform bonding quality.
Huawei is not the only Chinese chipmaker exploring hybrid bonding for advanced memory designs. According to the ISSCC 2027 agenda, ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT) will also present a 32Gb DDR5 SDRAM design with a data rate of 9.2Gb/s per pin, fabricated using vertical surrounding-gate transistors and W2W hybrid copper bonding.
China’s growing focus on vertical integration reflects its efforts to overcome manufacturing constraints. According to Sisa Journal, Choi Jae-hyuk, a professor at Seoul National University, said at an ISSCC 2027 press conference that limited access to advanced manufacturing equipment is driving Chinese companies toward alternatives such as hybrid bonding and LogicFolding. While these technologies may not pose an immediate commercial threat, expertise in vertical integration could offer long-term advantages.
Huawei Extends 3D Integration Efforts Beyond HBM
Huawei is also applying its 3D integration efforts to logic chips. STAR Market Daily reports that four Huawei papers are included in the ISSCC 2027 technical program, covering technologies ranging from custom HBM to a flagship mobile system-on-chip (SoC) featuring LogicFolding technology.
The conference will take place in San Francisco, U.S., from February 14 to 18, 2027, with all four Huawei presentations scheduled for February 16, EE Times China adds.

### 솔리다임, 240단 PLC 낸드 개발 '승부수'…AI 데이터센터 시장 선점 시동 - ZDNet korea

(https://vo.la/ZleQxMZ)

최근 SK하이닉스의 손자회사 솔리다임이 '펜타레벨셀(PLC)' 낸드 개발에서 유의미한 진전을 이뤄냈다. PLC는 메모리 셀 한 개에 5비트(bit)의 데이터를 저장하는 차세대 낸드플래시 기술로 기존 대비 고용량 낸드 구현에 유리해, 향후 AI 데이터센터 시장에서 수요가 증대될 것으로 기대된다.
26일 업계에 따르면 솔리다임은 내년 2월로 예정된 국제고체회로학회(ISSCC) 2027 학술대회에서 240단 적층 PLC 낸드 기술을 발표할 예정이다.
						
솔리다임, 셀 적층 경쟁 대신 'PLC'로 승부수
낸드는 셀(Cell; 데이터를 저장하는 최소 단위)을 수직으로 더 많이 쌓는 방식으로 진화해 왔다. 주요 낸드 제조기업인 삼성전자, SK하이닉스의 경우 각각 286단, 321단 낸드까지 양산화에 성공했다.
현재 솔리다임은 192단 제품을 양산하고 있다. 다음 양산 타겟도 240단으로 경쟁사 대비 적층 수는 낮지만, PLC 구조를 통해 차별점을 둘 것으로 전망된다.
PLC는 셀 하나에 5비트(bit)를 저장할 수 있다는 뜻이다. 비트는 0과 1로 구분되는 정보다. 예컨데 5비트는 00111, 10101 등 전압 상태에 따라 총 32개의 정보를 나타낼 수 있다. 때문에 3비트를 저장하는 트리플레벨셀(TLC), 4비트를 저장하는 쿼드레벨셀(QLC) 등에 비해 고용량 저장장치 구현에 유리하다.
다만 PLC는 기술적 난이도가 높아 아직까지 양산화 사례가 등장하지는 않았다. 셀 하나에서 32개의 전압 상태를 정밀하게 구분해야 하기 때문이다. 미세한 전하 변화 및 손실만으로도 인접한 전압 상태가 겹쳐 데이터 판독 오류가 발생할 수 있다. 삼성전자, SK하이닉스도 신뢰성을 이유로 TLC, QLC 낸드 양산에 그치고 있다.
						
경쟁사와 다른 플로팅게이트 구조…"삼성·SK 대비 PLC 구현 유리해"
그럼에도 업계는 솔리다임의 PLC 낸드 상용화 가능성을 비교적 높게 점치고 있다. 솔리다임이 주요 낸드 기업 중 유일하게 '플로팅 게이트(Floating Gate)'를 채택하고 있어서다. 삼성전자, SK하이닉스 등은 'CTF(차지 트랩 플래시)' 방식을 채택하고 있다.
플로팅게이트는 전기가 통하는 도체인 폴리실리콘 내부에 전하를 저장하는 방식이다. 각 셀마다 독립적인 전하 저장층을 가져, 안정적으로 전하를 보존할 수 있다. 0과 1 등 데이터 상태를 구분하기 위한 기준인 문턱전압도 일정하게 유지할 수 있다.
플로팅 게이트는 CTF 대비 셀 당 더 많은 비트를 저장하는 데에는 유리하다. 현재 솔리다임이 QLC 낸드 시장에서 상당한 강세를 보일 수 있는 이유다. 트랜드포스, 모건스탠리가 발간한 자료에 따르면 올해 QLC 낸드 출하량에서 SK하이닉스와 솔리다임의 합산 시장 점유율은 36%로 1위를 차지할 전망이다.
물론 단점도 있다. 플로팅 게이트는 CTF 대비 셀을 더 많이 쌓는 데에는 불리하다. CTF는 부도체의 국부적인 빈 공간(트랩)에 전하를 저장하는 구조로, 인접 셀로 전하가 이동하는 간섭 현상을 더 안정적으로 막아준다.
