### [News] Intel’s Mixed 3Q26: PC, Israel Fab Weigh on Guidance; EMIB Brightens as a Couple of Customers Near Announcement

[News] Intel’s Mixed 3Q26: PC, Israel Fab Weigh on Guidance; EMIB Brightens as a Couple of Customers Near Announcement
Intel’s 3Q26 earnings painted a mixed picture. While Data Center and AI (DCAI) revenue surged 78% to $7.3 billion and operating income more than tripled to $3.04 billion, the chipmaker remains vulnerable to weakness in the PC market. According to Reuters, a fatal explosion at Intel’s Israeli plant is expected to cost the company roughly $600 million in revenue over two quarters, while sluggish PC demand could persist through 1H27.
Reflecting these headwinds, Intel forecast fourth-quarter revenue of $15 billion to $16 billion, missing the $17.07 billion LSEG consensus, Reuters reports. Adjusted earnings are projected at 25 cents per share, also falling well short of analysts’ expectations of 43 cents, Reuters notes.
“I’m disappointed that we were not able to meet strong customer demand in this quarter,” CEO Lip-Bu Tan said, citing manufacturing supply shortages, an unprecedented memory crunch and the resulting weakness in PC demand as key near-term challenges.
Among the supply bottlenecks, Tan singled out advanced packaging materials as another constraint. According to Benzinga, he noted that despite rapidly ramping Intel 3 wafer output, an industry-wide shortage of advanced organic substrates for EMIB packaging continues to limit production scaling.
PC Sales Could Face 20% Plus Drop in 4Q26
The October explosion at Intel’s Kiryat Gat fab, which primarily produces data center processors using its 10nm technology, is dealing another blow. According to Reuters, CFO David Zinsner said the incident would reduce fourth-quarter revenue by roughly $300 million and shave 200 basis points off gross margin, with a similar revenue hit expected in 1Q27.
Following a two-week shutdown, Intel aims to begin restoring production at the Israeli facility by the end of this week, he added.
Meanwhile, weakness in the PC market is compounding the pressure. Zinsner expects Intel’s PC business revenue to plunge by a percentage in the mid-20s in the fourth quarter, after sales fell 5% to $8.10 billion in 3Q26, missing analysts’ $8.98 billion estimate, Reuters notes.
Aggressive capacity expansion is also adding to its financial strain. According to Reuters, Zinsner warned that the chipmaker is unlikely to generate positive cash flow in 2026. Intel had already raised its capital expenditure guidance from $18 billion to $20 billion in July, with a further substantial increase expected in 2027 to support its manufacturing buildout.
Despite these headwinds, Intel sees brighter prospects in the server CPU market. According to the earnings call transcript from Benzinga, CFO David Zinsner expects industry-wide server CPU unit shipments to grow more than 30% in both 2026 and 2027, pointing to robust demand beyond the struggling PC segment.
Meanwhile, Intel Foundry revenue surged 58% to $6.7 billion in 3Q26, including internal sales, while its operating loss narrowed to $1.85 billion from $2.32 billion a year earlier. The improvement points to progress in the foundry business despite continued losses.
Intel’s EMIB Making Strides
Notably, CNBC, citing Tan, reports that Intel expects a couple of customer announcements over the next two quarters, while touting “tremendous progress” on its upcoming 14A process technology. According to Tan, in October, the chipmaker delivered its industry-standard 0.9 process design kit (PDK) to select customers as planned.
In the transcript from Benzinga, Tan singled out the momentum for its EMIB and EMIB-T advanced packaging solutions, flagging that “We’re going to have a couple of customers ready to announce next two quarters, so stayed tuned.” According to Tan, Intel has got a lot of capacity in the next two years in terms of the substrate material, and it is also improving the yield as the days go by.
Customer interest is already translating into adoption. TrendForce expects Google to adopt EMIB-T in 2027, while AWS is also testing EMIB technology.
Industry sources earlier suggested Google’s TPU v9 lineup includes three variants: Broadcom-designed WhaleFish, and MediaTek-designed HumuFish and TriggerFish. WhaleFish is expected to adopt TSMC’s CoWoS-L, with mass production slated for 2H27. HumuFish, meanwhile, is set to enter mass production in 1H28, relying exclusively on Intel’s EMIB-T. TriggerFish is also planned to use EMIB-T, with production targeted for 2H28 or later.

### [News] MediaTek Confirms Second AI ASIC Customer for 2028 Production; First ASIC Shipments Begin in Sept.

(https://vo.la/iiktPx8)

[News] MediaTek Confirms Second AI ASIC Customer for 2028 Production; First ASIC Shipments Begin in Sept.
MediaTek reported double-digit revenue and profit growth in the third quarter of 2026. According to the company’s earnings release, consolidated revenue reached NT$168.06 billion, up 10.4% QoQ and 18.3% YoY, while gross margin remained stable at 46.3%. Net income rose 14.6% QoQ and 10.8% YoY to NT$28.20 billion, with earnings per share (EPS) of NT$17.50.
As noted by Central News Agency, MediaTek expects strong fourth-quarter growth driven by rising AI accelerator ASIC shipments. CEO Rick Tsai forecasts revenue of NT$210.1 billion to NT$221.8 billion, up 25% to 32% QoQ, with gross margin ranging from 43.5% to 46.5%. Full-year 2026 revenue growth in U.S. dollar terms is expected to exceed the company’s previous high-single-digit percentage target.
MediaTek Confirms Second AI ASIC Customer as Demand Grows
One of the key highlights of MediaTek’s earnings call was the confirmation of a second AI ASIC customer, marking a step toward diversifying its customer base, according to Mirror Media. While the new deal signals progress beyond a single major customer, MediaTek has yet to disclose the client’s identity, chip specifications or expected revenue.
Regarding market speculations that its second AI ASIC customer was ByteDance, MediaTek clarified that the customer is not a Chinese company. The new project is expected to enter mass production and begin contributing revenue in 2028, Mirror Media reports.
Meanwhile, Vice Chairman and CEO Rick Tsai said demand for MediaTek’s first AI accelerator remains strong, with small-volume shipments having begun in September. The company is confident of reaching US$2.5 billion in fourth-quarter revenue and the upper end of its previously stated 2027 revenue target range of approximately US$16 billion, equivalent to around 20% of the AI accelerator ASIC market, according to Mirror Media.
Second-Generation ASIC Tape-Out, Packaging Partnership in Focus
Addressing speculation about delays to its second-generation AI ASIC, Tsai said development remains on schedule. Several dies in the multi-die design have already completed tape-out, while larger, more complex dies, despite requiring several additional weeks, remain on track with the original timeline, as noted by ABmedia.
On advanced packaging, MediaTek is working closely with a U.S. integrated device manufacturer (IDM) and substrate suppliers to improve yields. Tsai said the team has identified key issues and potential solutions, expressing confidence that resolving two or three major problems could rapidly improve yields. Market observers believe the IDM partner is Intel and the technology is EMIB-T, according to Mirror Media.
The discussion over advanced packaging has also raised questions about MediaTek’s foundry strategy. Institutional investors questioned whether using Intel’s EMIB packaging could eventually require the company to adopt Intel’s foundry services. Tsai stressed that MediaTek does not rely on a single supplier and that TSMC remains its most important partner, with close cooperation across the supply chain, according to Taiwan’s Next Apple News.

상장 후 첫 배당 결정…총 480억 엔 규모
입력2026-10-30 23:07
인공지능(AI) 투자 열풍에 힘입어 급등하던 메모리 반도체 가격이 상승세를 늦추면서 일본 낸드플래시 업체 키옥시아홀딩스의 실적 전망이 시장 기대를 밑돌았다.
30일(현지 시간) 블룸버그통신에 따르면 키옥시아는 2026회계연도 3분기(10~12월) 영업이익이 2조 엔(약 16조 9770억 원)에 이를 것으로 전망했다. 이는 시장 예상치인 2조 3500억 엔을 크게 밑도는 수준이다. 2분기 낸드플래시 평균판매가격(ASP)은 전 분기 대비 20% 수준으로 상승하며 직전 분기의 상승률인 70%에서 크게 둔화했다.
메모리 가격 급등은 글로벌 메모리 반도체 기업의 수익성을 끌어올린 핵심 요인이었다. 그러나 멘주 아키라 키옥시아 재무부문 책임자는 “판매가격이 이미 상당히 상승한 상황에서 2027회계연도에도 가격이 계속 오를 수 있을지는 불분명하다”고 말했다. 블룸버그는 “키옥시아의 실적 전망은 지난 1년여간 이어진 이례적인 메모리 가격 급등세가 진정되기 시작했음을 시사한다”고 짚었다.
다만 키옥시아는 내년도 생산능력에 해당하는 물량의 공급처를 모두 확보했다고 밝혔다. 2028년 예상 출하량의 절반을 훨씬 넘는 물량도 하이퍼스케일러들과 장기 공급계약을 맺었다. 다만 이에 대해 애널리스트 사이에서는 의문도 제기됐다. 야스이 겐지 UBS증권 일본법인 리서치책임자는 경영진의 실적 전망 이후 “내년 회계연도에도 수급이 극도로 빠듯할 것이라고 하면서 가격 전망은 불확실한 게 맞느냐”고 물었다.
한편 AI 데이터센터 수요 증가에 힘입어 이날 발표된 키옥시아의 2026회계연도 상반기(4~9월) 누계 실적은 호조를 기록했다. 매출은 4조 2472억 엔으로 전년 동기(7911억 엔) 대비 436.8% 증가했다. 영업이익도 3조 2647억 엔으로 전년 동기(1308억 엔)의 25배였다. 영업이익률은 76.9%로 반도체 제조업체로서는 이례적인 수익성을 기록했다.
키옥시아는 첫 배당도 주당 90엔으로 실시하기로 했다. 당초 무배당을 계획했으나 실적 호조에 힘입어 상장 후 처음으로 배당을 실시하기로 한 것이다. 배당 총액은 약 480억 엔이다.
