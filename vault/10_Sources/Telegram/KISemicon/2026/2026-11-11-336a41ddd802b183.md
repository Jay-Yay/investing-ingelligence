### NVIDIA Reportedly Cancelled RTX 5080 24 GB As 3 GB GDDR7 Is Way Too Expensive, Eventually Leaving The 16 GB Variant As The Only Option

(https://vo.la/zUp7vb4)

We will likely won't see the higher VRAM variant for the RTX 5080 as NVIDIA is finding it difficult to secure 3 GB GDDR7 for cheap.
NVIDIA to Continue Production of the RTX 5080 16 GB Variant as 24 GB Version Gets Cancelled Due to Insanely Expensive 3 GB GDDR7 Modules
NVIDIA is reportedly making major changes to its plans for the RTX 50 series. Due to soaring VRAM prices, things have become difficult for the company to sustain enough VRAM chips for its graphics cards. Previously, it was reported that NVIDIA is going to discontinue the existing RTX 5080 16 GB variant and will replace it with the 24 GB version.
The latest report by Bobantang (Board Channels) suggests that NVIDIA has cancelled the 24 GB variant and won't continue with this plan anymore. As per the report, securing the higher-capacity GDDR7 memory modules has become incredibly difficult for the company. The RTX 5080 with 24 GB VRAM would use 3 GB GDDR7 memory modules instead of the 2 GB ones that are used in the entire RTX 50 series.
Various reports have previously confirmed that the cost of securing 3 GB GDDR7 memory chips is way more than the 2 GB ones, and this would have increased the price of the RTX 5080 24 GB significantly. In one report, we heard that the RTX 5080 24 GB could launch at nearly $2000, which would be twice the MSRP of the RTX 5080 16 GB Founders Edition. Since this is going to have an impact on sales, NVIDIA is no longer continuing with the original plan.
The report indicates that NVIDIA will now continue production of the RTX 5080 16 GB card, which was rumored to have been cancelled recently. We saw the RTX 5080 being delisted from the official NVIDIA marketplace, but it's usual for the company to list and delist GPUs. So, even though the RTX 5080's availability remains low at retailers, it's still going to be in production.
However, the RTX 5080 could soon hit $3000 as we recently reported that MSI is increasing its price by up to a whopping $1200. So, launching the more expensive 24 GB variant could have been disastrous.
