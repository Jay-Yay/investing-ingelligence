### SK하이닉스, HBF 첫 표준규격 공개…AI 메모리 선점 시동 - ZDNet korea

(https://vo.la/37L2hAd)

SK하이닉스가 인공지능(AI) 데이터센터용 고부가 메모리 개발에 속도를 낸다. 이달 미국 주요 학회에서 고대역폭플래시(HBF)의 첫 표준 규격과, 375단 적층 10세대 낸드를 최초로 선보였다.
SK하이닉스는 샌디스크와 함께 차세대 스토리지 기술인 HBF의 첫 표준 규격을 공개했다고 4일 밝혔다.
이번 발표는 4~6일(현지시간) 미국 캘리포니아주 산타클라라 컨벤션 센터에서 열리는 'FMS(Future of Memory and Storage) 2026' 개막에 맞춰 이뤄졌다. SK하이닉스는 이번 행사 키노트 연설과 패널 토의를 통해 HBF를 AI 시대 메모리 병목을 풀어낼 핵심 기술로 소개할 계획이다.
HBF는 고대역폭메모리(HBM)와 솔리드스테이트드라이브(SSD) 사이에 놓는 새로운 메모리 계층이다. HBM처럼 데이터를 빠르게 주고받으면서도, 낸드를 기반으로 해 용량을 크게 키울 수 있다. AI 추론 확산으로 다뤄야 할 데이터가 급증한 상황에서, 대역폭과 용량 확장성을 동시에 잡을 수 있는 기술로 주목받고 있다.
이번 규격은 SK하이닉스가 지난해 8월 샌디스크와 HBF 표준화 협력에 나선 데 이어, 올해 2월 컨소시엄을 출범한 지 약 6개월 만에 내놓은 첫 결과물이다. 용량은 낸드 다이를 8단과 16단으로 쌓는 두 가지 스택 구성을 기준으로 최대 512GB까지 정의했다. 대역폭은 세 등급(그레이드 1~3)으로 나눠 약 0.4TB/s에서 3.0TB/s까지 구현할 수 있도록 규정했다.
HBF와 프로세서를 잇는 연결 방식은 업계 표준인 UCIe를 채택했다. 그래픽처리장치(GPU)·중앙처리장치(CPU) 등 종류가 다른 프로세서에도 HBF를 유연하게 연결해 쓸 수 있는 기반을 마련했다. UCIe는 서로 다른 반도체 칩렛을 고속 연결하기 위한 개방형 표준 인터페이스 규격이다.
회사는 이를 계기로 AI 스토리지 시장에서 HBF 채택을 확대하고, 생태계 조성에도 속도를 낸다는 방침이다. 현재 HBF 컨소시엄에는 구글, 텐스토렌트가 참여하고 있다. 개방형 협력을 바탕으로 저변을 넓히고 기술 완성도와 시장 수용성을 함께 끌어올릴 계획이다.

### HP, Asus and Acer reportedly turn to CXMT for laptop memory

(https://vo.la/d4LTYvX)

HP, Asus and Acer have begun using limited quantities of DRAM from China's ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT) in selected budget notebooks sold outside the US, Nikkei Asia reported, as a global memory shortage pushes PC makers to widen their supplier base.
Many major PC makers completed qualification of CXMT's DRAM around the middle of 2026 before beginning limited adoption. The chips are being used in only a small number of models, while CXMT continues to direct much of its production to Chinese customers such as Huawei.
PC makers widen their sourcing as shortages worsen
PC makers are proceeding cautiously because Samsung Electronics, SK Hynix and Micron continue to dominate global DRAM supply. Buyers are reluctant to strain relationships with the three incumbents while memory remains a seller's market.
Lower prices are not necessarily the attraction. One person familiar with the matter said CXMT's DRAM was no cheaper than comparable Samsung products, while customers were unable to secure supplies beyond the current quarter because of strong demand.
Acer did not confirm whether it was using CXMT products. The company said it remains in contact with multiple global manufacturers and suppliers to manage component price changes and strengthen supply chain resilience. Asus declined to comment, while HP and CXMT did not respond.
The adoption comes as memory constraints weigh on the broader PC market. IDC expects global PC shipments to decline 11.3% in 2026, with fourth-quarter shipments potentially falling 20% from a year earlier. The research firm does not expect meaningful relief from the shortage before the end of 2027 and forecasts that average PC selling prices will rise 18.3% this year.
Component scarcity and rising prices are also making it increasingly difficult for manufacturers to maintain complete product portfolios.
CXMT pushes ahead with capacity buildout
Reuters reported that CXMT is considering constructing a second memory-chip plant in Beijing and is in early financing talks with a technology manufacturing hub backed by the local government.
The proposal has not been finalized, and the size and structure of any funding package could change.
CXMT currently operates two 12-inch DRAM fabs in Hefei and one in Beijing, each with monthly capacity of approximately 100,000 wafers. New projects in Shanghai and Hefei, together with another facility under discussion, could more than double its total capacity to above 600,000 wafers per month when fully operational.
CXMT ranks as the world's fourth-largest DRAM producer but remains substantially smaller than Samsung, SK Hynix and Micron. The three incumbents together held close to 90% of the market in the first quarter, based on Counterpoint Research data.
The expansion follows CXMT's US$8.6 billion initial public offering in July, the largest mainland Chinese semiconductor listing on record.
CXMT has also expanded its PC-oriented product portfolio. The company showcased DDR5 and LPDDR5X memory products in November 2025. Its DDR5 lineup reaches speeds of up to 8,000 Mbps and includes SODIMM and CSODIMM modules, while the broader portfolio targets servers, workstations and personal computers.
Washington scrutiny complicates CXMT's overseas push
CXMT's entry into the global PC supply chain faces political scrutiny in Washington.
In a July 14 letter, House Select Committee on China Chairman John Moolenaar and Representative George Whitesides urged Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick to expedite a formal review of whether CXMT should be added to the Commerce Department's Entity List.
The lawmakers noted that the Defense Department has designated CXMT under Section 1260H as a Chinese military company. CXMT has denied allegations of military ties. The Section 1260H designation is separate from placement on the Commerce Department's Entity List.
Yangtze Memory Technologies (YMTC) is already on the Entity List. The lawmakers asked the Commerce Department to strengthen restrictions on YMTC while adding CXMT, which is not currently listed.
They also proposed guidance that would prohibit US persons and US-incorporated entities from procuring DRAM, high-bandwidth memory and other memory components from CXMT, YMTC or any company on the Entity List or Section 1260H list. This would apply to use in AI systems, data centers, federal information technology and critical infrastructure.
The lawmakers called for coordination with South Korea, Japan and the European Union. The measures are policy recommendations and have not become Commerce Department rules.
