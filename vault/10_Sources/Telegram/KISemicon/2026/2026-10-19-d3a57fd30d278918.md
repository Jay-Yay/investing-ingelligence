### [News] MediaTek’s Google TPU v9 Shipments May Slip to 1Q28; ASIC Race Heats Up as Taiwan Firms Eye Opportunities

(https://vo.la/8Vu5kgO)

[News] MediaTek’s Google TPU v9 Shipments May Slip to 1Q28; ASIC Race Heats Up as Taiwan Firms Eye Opportunities
Taiwan’s ASIC design industry is seeing growing opportunities, with MediaTek emerging as a key player in the expanding market. However, according to Commercial Times, citing institutional investors, Google’s TPU v9 project, involving MediaTek, could see its tape-out delayed until January 2027 following design revisions, with shipments potentially pushed back to the first quarter of 2028.
The report notes that revisions to Google’s TPU v9 Core A design, codenamed Humufish, have delayed development by approximately three weeks, affecting the Core B and I/O designs handled by MediaTek. Although the November 2026 sampling target remains unchanged, the initial tape-out is now expected in January 2027, followed by the production-version tape-out in the second quarter.
Beyond the design revisions, Google’s TPU v9 project is also drawing attention for its advanced packaging strategy. According to TrendForce, the chip is expected to adopt Intel’s EMIB-T, with substrate yields remaining a key challenge to production scalability and cost competitiveness.
MediaTek is also seeking to expand its ASIC customer base beyond Google. According to Bloomberg, citing sources, ByteDance is nearing an agreement to purchase MediaTek’s data center chips, potentially making the TikTok parent the Taiwanese chipmaker’s second major ASIC customer after Alphabet’s Google. Bloomberg notes that securing a major ByteDance contract would support MediaTek’s efforts to diversify beyond smartphones and strengthen its position against Qualcomm and Broadcom in the data center market.
Taiwan’s ASIC Firms See Revenue Growth and New Foundry Opportunities
Beyond MediaTek, other Taiwanese ASIC design companies are seeing advanced chip projects translate into stronger revenue. Global Unichip Corporation (GUC), supported by cloud customers’ CPU and ASIC programs, reported record September revenue of NT$7.145 billion, up 97.8% year over year. Alchip Technologies posted September revenue of NT$9.979 billion, surging 496% year over year as shipments of 3nm AI accelerators increased.
Faraday Technology is also expanding its capabilities across multiple foundry platforms, positioning itself for opportunities with Samsung and Intel. According to Commercial Times, Samsung’s 4nm capacity is reportedly fully utilized, prompting some AI ASIC customers to evaluate its 2nm node. Faraday has obtained Samsung’s 2nm process design kit (PDK) to pursue related design opportunities. The company has also completed its design environment setup for Intel’s 18A-P and begun offering design services, with plans to pursue more projects in 2027.
According to Commercial Times, citing institutional investors, global AI ASIC accelerator shipments are projected to reach 14.1 million units in 2027, up approximately 69% year over year, highlighting growing opportunities for ASIC design companies.

### 앰코코리아, 광주사업장에 첨단패키징 증설…2031년까지 3조 투입 - ZDNet korea

(https://vo.la/PAN9yxu)

첨단 반도체패키징 전문기업 앰코테크놀로지코리아가 2031년까지 광주사업장에 3조원을 투입, 인공지능(AI)·고성능컴퓨팅(HPC) 첨단 패키징 공장 2개동을 완공하고 양산에 나선다.
이민우 산업통상부 산업성장실장은 19일 앰코코리아 광주사업장에서 열린 ‘앰코코리아 광주공장 증축 착공식’에서 서남권 첨단반도체 투자 이행을 적극 지원하겠다고 밝혔다.
앰코코리아의 증축은 지난 6월 30일 ‘서남권 첨단산업 발전비전 국민보고회’에서 발표된 앰코의 광주 투자계획이 본격적인 이행 단계에 들어섰음을 의미한다.
앰코코리아는 이날 착공식에서 첨단 패키징 수요 확대에 대응해 광주사업장에 올해부터 2031년까지 총 3조원을 투자하고 약 2000명을 고용할 계획이라고 발표했다. 애초 계획보다 투자 규모가 2조원 늘고 고용은 약 1000명 증가했다.
새로 짓는 공장은 AI·HPC용 첨단 패키징 제품을 생산한다. 내년 하반기에 2개동, 2028년 상반기에 2개동을 완공하고 양산 개시를 목표로 잡았다.
산업부 관계자는 “첨단 패키징은 서로 다른 칩을 하나로 쌓고 연결해 반도체의 성능과 전력효율을 높이는 기술”이라며 “회로 미세화가 물리적 한계에 다가가면서 AI 반도체 경쟁력을 좌우하는 핵심 기술로 떠오르고 있다”고 설명했다.

이민우 산업부 산업성장실장은 “오늘 착공은 서남권 반도체 투자가 계획에서 현장으로 옮겨가는 첫 신호이자, 글로벌 기업이 대한민국과 광주의 미래를 믿고 내린 결정”이라며 “AI 시대 반도체 경쟁력을 좌우하는 첨단 패키징 공장이 광주에 들어서는 만큼, 반도체 생태계가 서남권에 뿌리내릴 수 있도록 힘을 보태겠다”고 말했다.
