### NVIDIA Vera CPU Cruise Past Intel's Xeon 6 In Database Benchmark With Fewer Cores, Delivering 48% Faster Query Performance

(https://vo.la/Z6SVHxI)

NVIDIA's Vera CPU has been tested in the TCP-H benchmark, achieving faster performance than its x86 rivals.
88-Core Vera vs 96-Core Xeon 6 CPU Showdown Unveils NVIDIA Leading In Databases & Queries
DuckDB recently got access to NVIDIA's Vera CPU and has been testing its capabilities through the full TPC-H workload. The testing pits the 88-Core NVIDIA Vera chip against a 96-core Xeon 6 chip by Intel.
What's important is that this isn't a AI benchmark but a traditional database/query suite. The following is what the creators of the TPC-H benchmark have to say themselves about the test:
The TPC-H is a decision support benchmark. It consists of a suite of business oriented ad-hoc queries and concurrent data modifications. The queries and the data populating the database have been chosen to have broad industry-wide relevance. This benchmark illustrates decision support systems that examine large volumes of data, execute queries with a high degree of complexity, and give answers to critical business questions.
The performance metric reported by TPC-H is called the TPC-H Composite Query-per-Hour Performance Metric (QphH@Size), and reflects multiple aspects of the capability of the system to process queries. These aspects include the selected database size against which the queries are executed, the query processing power when queries are submitted by a single stream, and the query throughput when queries are submitted by multiple concurrent users. The TPC-H Price/Performance metric is expressed as Price/QphH@Size for Version 2 & Price/kQphH@Size for Version 3.
The 96-Core Intel Xeon 6 with Hyper-Threading was used as a baseline. This setup featured 768 GB of memory, a 3 TB+ NVMe SSD for storage, and was running on the Ubuntu 26.04 operating system. The NVIDIA Vera 88-Core chip was running with Spatial Multi-Threading with 768 GB of LPDDR5x memory, 3 TB+ NVMe SSD storage, and the same operating system.
Tests were conducted using the existing DuckDB v1.5.5 release and the DuckDB v2.0.0-Alpha release. The benchmark results can be seen below:
First of all, the initial warm-up run was excluded, and the following three runs were conducted to measure a median value of the QphH@SF composite scores.
As per the table, the 88-Core NVIDIA Vera CPUs delivered 46-48% better performance than the Intel Xeon 6 chip across both DuckDB releases. The Alpha version sees a 24-26% boost across both chips, so this performance will be available to all users running DuckDB.
What these benchmarks show us is that NVIDIA Vera pulled ahead in a classic database workload against x86 offerings. The advantage for Vera isn't simply AI; it's a broad range of traditional HPC and Enterprise workloads as evaluated in DuckDB’s TPC-H tests.
On equal memory and storage, fewer cores and Spatial Multi-Threading, Vera still delivered higher query throughput. For analytics and decision-support databases, Vera is a very performant chip.

### [News] TSMC Plans Mini-Loop at Kaohsiung to Speed CoWoS Tool Validation; Reportedly Eyes AUO Site for CoPoS

(https://vo.la/8jHqGOo)

[News] TSMC Plans Mini-Loop at Kaohsiung to Speed CoWoS Tool Validation; Reportedly Eyes AUO Site for CoPoS
As AI demand drives growth in advanced packaging, TSMC is strengthening equipment and materials validation across its supply chain. According to Commercial Times, construction of Kaohsiung’s Baipu Industrial Park began on September 21, with TSMC expected to anchor the cluster. The company plans to set up a modular “Mini-Loop” line to validate new equipment and process integration before the equipment is introduced into CoWoS (Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate) production, aiming to improve validation efficiency by 25% to 50%.
As the report highlights, the move could signal a more direct role for TSMC in coordinating its supply chain. TSMC Vice President of Advanced Packaging Technology and Service Jun He estimates that CoWoS capacity will grow at a compound annual rate of more than 80% through 2027, with growth continuing through 2029. Unlike major front-end equipment makers, many back-end equipment suppliers are smaller and have more limited R&D resources, making it increasingly difficult for them to conduct equipment and process validation independently as AI chips evolve rapidly.
Meanwhile, ASE reportedly plans to build two facilities at Baipu after breaking ground on a new Renwu facility in April. If the investment moves forward, it would bring TSMC’s equipment and materials validation platform closer to ASE’s packaging and testing capabilities, as the report points out.
TrendForce notes that GPU suppliers and hyperscale CSPs are continuing to develop increasingly powerful and versatile AI chips, making larger package sizes a key development priority for 2.5D packaging. Although TSMC’s CoWoS-L faces competition from Intel’s EMIB-T, its advantages in technology maturity and yield are expected to keep it the mainstream packaging solution for AI chips through 2028.
TSMC Explores Glass Substrates as CoPoS Packaging Scales Up
TSMC is also reportedly exploring technologies for next-generation CoPoS as AI chips grow in size. According to another report from Commercial Times, the company is working to shift packaging substrates from round wafers to square panels while developing glass-core substrates. TSMC’s 3DIC R&D center in Japan is already developing glass-substrate technologies involving through-glass vias (TGVs), redistribution layers (RDLs), and GPU and HBM integration.
According to a July report from Economic Daily News, TSMC is currently building a CoPoS pilot line, with the technology and yields expected to take about another year to mature before production is introduced with customers.
Notably, TSMC has recently made multiple visits to AUO’s Central Taiwan Science Park facility, reportedly involving both a potential facility transaction and its CoPoS plans. The Commercial Times report notes that AUO’s experience in large-format panel processing, glass fabrication, and factory infrastructure overlaps with some of the capabilities required for panel-level packaging.
CoPoS Push Puts TGV and Inspection in Focus
For glass substrates, TGV formation is expected to become a key area of supply-chain competition. As Commercial Times highlights, the market currently favors ultrafast lasers combined with wet etching, using picosecond or femtosecond lasers to modify the glass before chemical etching forms the vias. Inspection is also expected to be a critical step in CoPoS mass production. The report notes that microcracks formed during laser drilling, copper plating, or thermal processing could spread to adjacent TGVs, affecting electrical performance and packaging yields.

### [단독] 구형칩 임대료마저 25%↑...AI 연산비용 ‘쓰나미’ 오나 | 서울경제

단독구형칩 임대료마저 25%↑...AI 연산비용 ‘쓰나미’ 오나
수요 급증에 4년 된 H100 몸값 역주행
업스테이지 고객사로 둔 베슬AI 등
국내 네오클라우드도 이용료 줄인상
인공지능(AI) 연산 수요 급증에 따라 코어위브·네비우스 등 미국 네오클라우드 기업들이 최근 연이어 그래픽처리장치(GPU) 이용료 인상을 단행한 가운데 국내 기업 베슬AI도 이달부터 엔비디아 H100 이용료를 24.7% 인상한 것으로 확인됐다. 최근 고성능 GPU 수요가 급증하면서 최신 제품뿐 아니라 구형 모델까지 물량 확보가 어려워진 탓이다. 국내 다른 클라우드 업체들도 수급 상황에 따라 GPU 이용료 인상을 전망하고 있어 GPU 확보 비용 상승이 국내 AI 기업의 서비스 개발과 정부 인공지능 전환(AX) 사업 등에 부담이 될 것이라는 우려가 나온다.
21일 AI 업계에 따르면 베슬AI는 이달 1일 자체 클라우드 서비스 ‘VESSL Cloud’의 H100 시간당 이용료를 기존 2.39달러에서 2.98달러로 인상했다. 베슬AI는 업스테이지·모티프테크놀로지스 등 국내 주요 AI 기업을 고객으로 두고 현재 수천 장 규모의 GPU를 운영하는 네오클라우드 기업이다. 회사는 현재 약 7000장의 GPU를 확보하고 있으며 이 중 대부분을 직접 구매하기보다 임대 방식으로 조달하고 있다. 베슬AI 관계자는 “H100은 사실상 ‘부르는 게 가격’이라고 할 정도로 필요한 물량을 확보하기 쉽지 않다”며 “AI 수요가 늘면서 GPU를 이용하려는 고객은 계속 증가하고 있어 가격 조정이 불가피했다”고 상황을 설명했다. 베슬AI는 연말까지 GPU 운영 규모를 1만 장 규모로 확대할 계획이지만 향후에도 GPU 수급 상황과 조달 비용 등을 반영해 이용료를 탄력적으로 조정할 방침이다.
베슬AI뿐만 아니라 앨리스 등 다른 국내 AI 클라우드 기업도 최근 가격을 인상하고 있다. 특히 과거 AI용 GPU는 신제품이 나오면 구형 제품의 임대료가 낮아졌지만 최근에는 이전 세대 제품의 가격도 함께 오르는 추세다. AI 연산 거래 플랫폼 온(Ornn)에 따르면 H100의 시간당 클라우드 대여료는 최근 한 달 사이 2.69달러에서 3.28달러로 21.9% 상승했다. 상위 모델인 H200 가격의 약 70% 수준이다. 최신 GPU 공급이 급증하는 연산 수요를 따라가지 못하면서 상대적으로 오래된 GPU로까지 수요가 확산하고 있다는 분석이다.
이러한 흐름은 국내 AI 산업과 연구 현장에도 영향을 미친다. 일부 기업들은 필요한 GPU를 확보하지 못해 정부가 추진하는 AX 사업 입찰을 포기하는 것으로 나타났다. 대학·연구기관 역시 GPU를 안정적으로 확보하지 못해 연구 규모를 줄이거나 새로운 지원 사업을 찾아다니는 상황이다. 한 AI 업계 관계자는 “이제는 좋은 AI 기술을 갖고 있느냐뿐 아니라 필요한 GPU를 제때 확보할 수 있느냐가 사업과 연구의 성패를 좌우하는 상황”이라며 “연산자원 확보 능력이 AI 경쟁력의 중요한 요소가 되고 있다”고 말했다. ▷기사2면
