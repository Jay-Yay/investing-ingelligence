 Shopify Sustains Rapid Growth with Revenue Up 32% and Free Cash Flow Margin of 22% 

 November 3, 2026 - Shopify announced today financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Shopify achieved 32% revenue growth (32% in constant currency) and a 22% free cash flow margin. 

 “Shopify is uncommonly good at operating in two realities at the same time: the world our merchants exist in today, and the one they are moving towards. Q3 is the perfect example. We powered $119 billion in GMV, while continuing to build commerce into every surface, every agent, and increasingly, everywhere,” said Harley Finkelstein, President of Shopify. 

 “We extended our track record of consistent execution, with 32% revenue growth and a 22% free cash flow margin,”
said Jeff Hoffmeister, Chief Financial Officer of Shopify. “Delivering these results at our scale speaks to the strength of our model, our multiple growth drivers, and our expanding addressable market.”

 Endnotes: 

 Gross Merchandise Volume, or GMV, represents the total dollar value of orders facilitated through the Shopify platform including certain apps and channels for which a revenue-sharing arrangement is in place in the period, net of refunds, and inclusive of shipping and handling, duty, and value-added taxes. 

 Monthly Recurring Revenue , or MRR, is the aggregate value of all subscription plans, excluding variable platform fees, in effect on the last day of the period, assuming merchants maintain their subscription the following month and is used by management as a directional indicator of subscription solutions revenue going forward. 

 Free cash flow and free cash flow margin are non-GAAP financial measures that are reconciled in the non-GAAP reconciliation within this press release. These non-GAAP financial measures do not have standardized meanings under US GAAP and may not be comparable to similar measures presented by other companies. Shopify believes free cash flow and free cash flow margin provide useful information to help investors and others understand our operating results and the performance of our business in the same manner as management. Shopify does not reconcile forward-looking non-GAAP free cash flow margin because certain financial information, the probable significance of which cannot be determined, is not available and cannot be reasonably estimated. Free cash flow is a non-GAAP financial measure calculated as cash flow from operations less capital expenditures. 

 Reconciliation of GAAP Net Income to Non-GAAP Net Income provides supplementary non-GAAP financial measures for non-GAAP net income and diluted net income per share to consider in evaluating operating performance. Non-GAAP adjustments to net income include stock-based compensation expense and related payroll taxes, amortization of acquired intangible assets, impairment of right-of-use assets and leaseholds, net loss on equity and other investments, net loss on equity method investment, loss on embedded derivative, and income tax effects and tax adjustments, including a release of valuation allowance as a discrete tax benefit. These non-GAAP measures should not be viewed as a substitute for reported results determined in accordance with GAAP, nor are they necessarily comparable to non-GAAP measures that may be presented by other companies.
