 Applied Optoelectronics
Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results 

 

 Sugar Land, Texas, November 5, 2026 – Applied Optoelectronics,
Inc. (NASDAQ: AAOI) (“AOI”), a leading provider of advanced optical and HFC networking products that power AI, today announced
financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

“We are pleased to have generated record revenue for our sixth
consecutive quarter and to have returned to GAAP profitability in Q3,” said Dr. Thompson Lin, AOI’s Founder, President and
Chief Executive Officer. “We have made meaningful progress in scaling our manufacturing capabilities, particularly for our 800G
and 1.6T products, to address demand for AI-related optical technologies that we believe is accelerating. We have recently secured a multi-year
strategic agreement with a Tier 1 hyperscale datacenter customer, which we think provides us with improved long-term visibility and reinforces
our confidence in the opportunity ahead as demand for high-bandwidth, energy-efficient optical connectivity products continues to grow.”

 

“We delivered another solid quarter, including accelerating revenue
growth and improving profitability, driven by robust demand and manufacturing expansion in both our datacenter and CATV businesses,”
said Dr. Stefan Murry, AOI’s Chief Financial Officer and Chief Strategy Officer. “We currently have a total manufacturing
equipment capacity approaching 330,000 units per month. We expect our 2027 capacity expansion plan in the US to take longer to complete
due to longer than expected time to obtain necessary electrical power among other construction related issues. As we work through these
construction issues, we now expect by the end of 2026 that we will have sufficient manufacturing equipment installed to produce over 500,000
pieces of 800G and 1.6 Tb products per month and we expect to grow our manufacturing capacity to over 1 million pieces of 800G and 1.6
Tb transceivers per month by the end of 2027. We remain focused on executing on our capacity expansion plans and believe we are well positioned
to support the next phase of AI datacenter deployment.”

 

 Third Quarter 2026 Financial Summary 

 

 

 · GAAP revenue was $269.9 million, compared with $118.6 million in the third
quarter of 2025 and $191.9 million in the second quarter of 2026. 
 

 

 

 · GAAP gross margin was 28.2%, compared with 28.0% in the third quarter of
2025 and 27.7% in the second quarter of 2026. Non-GAAP gross margin was 28.7%, compared with 31.0% in the third quarter of 2025 and 29.8%
in the second quarter of 2026. 
 

 

 

 · GAAP net income was $6.3 million, or $0.07 per basic share, compared with
net loss of $17.9 million, or $0.28 per basic share in the third quarter of 2025, and a net loss of $22.8 million, or $0.28 per basic
share in the second quarter of 2026. 
 

 

 

 · Non-GAAP net income was $14.8 million, or $0.17 per diluted share, compared
with non-GAAP net loss of $5.4 million, or $0.09 per basic share in the third quarter of 2025, and a non-GAAP net income of $5.5 million,
or $0.06 per diluted share in the second quarter of 2026.

 Non-GAAP Financial Measures 

 

We provide non-GAAP gross margin, non-GAAP net income (loss), and non-GAAP
earnings (loss) per share, and non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA to eliminate the impact of items that we do not consider indicative of our overall
operating performance. To arrive at our non-GAAP gross margin, we exclude stock-based compensation expenses, expenses associated with
discontinued products, and non-recurring (income) expenses, if any, from our GAAP gross margin. To arrive at our non-GAAP net income (loss),
we exclude all amortization of intangible assets, stock-based compensation expense, expenses associated with discontinued products, non-cash
expenses associated with discontinued products and non-recurring (income) expenses, unrealized foreign exchange loss (gain), and non-GAAP
tax benefit (expenses) from our GAAP net income (loss). Included in our non-recurring expenses in Q3 2026 and Q3 2025 are employee severance
expenses (if any) and legal expenses associated with litigation and certain legal and advisory expenses associated with purchase termination
or patent protection. In computing our non-GAAP income tax benefit (expense), we have applied an estimate of our annual effective income
tax rate and applied it to our net income before income taxes. Our non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA is calculated by excluding depreciation expense,
non-GAAP tax benefit (expense), and interest (income) expense, as well as the items excluded from non-GAAP net income (loss), from our
GAAP net income (loss). Our non-GAAP diluted net earnings (loss) per share is calculated by dividing our non-GAAP net gain (loss) by the
fully diluted share count (for periods in which non-GAAP net income is positive) or basic share count (for periods in which our non-GAAP
net income is negative).

 

We believe that our non-GAAP measures are useful to investors in evaluating
our operating performance for the following reasons:

 

| · | We believe that elimination of items such as amortization of intangible assets, stock-based compensation expense, non-recurring revenue and expenses, losses from the disposal of idle assets, unrealized foreign exchange gain or loss, and depreciation on certain equipment undergoing reconfiguration is appropriate because treatment of these items may vary for reasons unrelated to our overall operating performance; |
| --- | --- |
| · | We believe that elimination of expenses associated with discontinued products, including depreciation and inventory obsolescence is appropriate because these expenses are not indicative of our ongoing operations; |
| · | We believe that estimating non-GAAP income taxes allows comparison with prior periods and provides additional information regarding the generation of potential future deferred tax assets; |
| · | We believe that non-GAAP measures provide better comparability with our past financial performance, period-to-period results and with our peer companies, many of which also use similar non-GAAP financial measures; and |
| · | We anticipate that investors and securities analysts will utilize non-GAAP measures as a supplement to GAAP measures to evaluate our overall operating performance. |

 About Applied Optoelectronics 

 

 Applied Optoelectronics,
Inc. (AOI) is a leading developer and manufacturer of advanced optical and HFC networking products that serve as the critical building
blocks for AI datacenters, CATV and broadband fiber access networks worldwide. AOI supplies these critical infrastructure products to
tier-one customers across cloud computing, CATV broadband, telecommunications, and FTTH markets. The company has R&D facilities in
Atlanta, GA, and engineering and manufacturing facilities at its corporate headquarters in Sugar Land, TX, as well as in Taipei, Taiwan
and Ningbo, China. For additional information, visit www.ao-inc.com . #
