 3 Assumptions and Notes 1. Total AI Cloud Revenue comprises AI Cloud Services Revenue of $135m and AI Cloud Lease Revenue of $65m (prior quarter: $71m and nil, respectively), per our unaudited condensed consolidated statements of operations. 2. Adjusted EBITDA and Total segment gross profit are non-GAAP financial measures. AI Cloud segment gross margin is AI Cloud segment gross profit (Total AI Cloud revenue less AI Cloud cost of revenue, excluding depreciation and amortization and before site-level costs) divided by Total AI Cloud revenue. Refer to the reconciliations at the end of this press release to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measures. 3. Revenue backlog includes remaining performance obligations plus remaining lease arrangements plus other amounts we estimate may be recognized as revenue in future periods under committed customer contracts, in each case, subject to the satisfaction of delivery and availability of service requirements. Investment-grade share is based on the credit ratings of counterparties or their guarantors as of September 30, 2026. 4. Annualized-run-rate revenue (“ARR”) is calculated as GPU/hour pricing for commissioned GPUs under contract as of the relevant date multiplied by 8,760 hours per year and includes annualized revenue for storage and ancillaries. More than $4 billion of ARR is targeted to be operational by January 31, 2027, and may extend to the end of February 2027, based on internal company assumptions regarding utilization and pricing, with revenue expected to ramp upon, and being subject to, commissioning, testing and customer acceptance in the months following each data center’s delivery. ARR is not derived from or an adjustment to GAAP revenue, and excludes GAAP recognition and measurement effects. ARR is an operating metric, not a U.S. GAAP measure, and differs from, and is not a substitute for, GAAP revenue; recognized revenue may be materially lower. 5. Cash and committed GPU financing and prepayments comprises cash and cash equivalents, restricted cash, undrawn committed GPU financing facilities and contracted customer prepayments not yet received, as of September 30, 2026. “Fully funded” means these sources cover the remaining capex from September 30, 2026, including data centers and GPUs, for the 0.3GW (IT) program that supports the more than $4 billion ARR target, subject to satisfaction of the conditions to funding under committed financing facilities, including conditions to the release of escrowed proceeds and applicable availability periods. 6. Estimated average annual revenue per MW (IT) for 3- and 5-year contracts signed during Q1 FY27 and Q4 FY26 based on internal assumptions on power consumption for each contracted deployment, weighted by ARR. 7. Reflects contracts signed after Q1 FY27. Revenue per IT-MW is estimated annual revenue per MW (IT). Customer prepayment coverage reflects customer prepayments as a proportion of associated estimated GPU capex for the five-year contract. Customer prepayments represent amounts contractually payable by customers in advance of service delivery, expressed as a percentage of the estimated capital expenditure attributable to the associated deployments. Prepayment terms vary by contract and there can be no assurance that future contracts will include prepayments or similar terms. GPU capex excludes provincial sales tax and goods and services tax. H100 pricing increase is relative to pricing under the prior H100 contract at Prince George. 8. Includes land secured in freehold and under option, as of October 31, 2026. 9. >35GW development pipeline represents the potential gross power capacity, as of November 5, 2026, of projects at various stages of development prior to execution of grid connection agreements, letters of agreement or equivalents. These projects are speculative, and we do not currently expect a significant portion of them to result in secured power. There can be no assurance that any project will proceed or that the indicated capacity will ultimately be secured or developed. 10. Data center assets comprise buildings, construction in progress and land, net of depreciation, as of September 30, 2026. 11. Remaining capex is from September 30, 2026 and includes data centers and GPUs. 12. Future capacity targets include further capacity across the platform’s secured power and development pipeline, including the 0.8GW of data center capacity by the end of calendar 2027, which may require additional capex in FY27.

Special Note Regarding Non-GAAP Measures This press release refers to certain financial measures not recognized under generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (“GAAP”), including Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA margin, Total segment gross profit, Net debt and Cash operating expense. Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA margin Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income (loss), excluding income tax (expense) benefit, finance expense, interest income, depreciation and amortization, stock-based compensation expense, foreign exchange gain (loss), impairment of assets, certain other non-recurring income, gain (loss) on disposal of property, plant and equipment, unrealized fair value gain (loss) on financial

Adjusted EBITDA Margin is defined as Adjusted EBITDA divided by revenue. Total segment gross profit Total segment gross profit is the sum of AI Cloud and Bitcoin Mining segment revenue less each segment’s cost of revenue, excluding depreciation and amortization, consistent with Note 3 to the unaudited condensed consolidated financial statements in our Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. The aggregate is a non-GAAP measure outside the segment footnote. It helps assess segment contribution before depreciation and amortization, corporate and other expenses. Net debt Net debt is current and non-current debt at GAAP carrying value, less unrestricted cash and cash equivalents and less current and non-current restricted cash. It excludes operating lease liabilities. It helps assess borrowings net of cash balances. Restricted cash includes amounts held in escrow accounts under our debt financing and letter of credit arrangements and remains subject to contractual limits on withdrawal or use. Cash operating expense Cash operating expense is GAAP selling, general and administrative expense and GAAP research and development expense, less stock-based compensation. It helps assess SG&A and R&D before stock-based compensation and is not a measure of actual cash payments. Forward-looking Cash operating expense uses the same definition. - 10-K/10-Q/20-F는 primaryDocument HTML에서 텍스트를 추출해 전문을 캡처한다(태그만 제거한 단순 변환이라 표 구조가 완벽히 보존되진 않는다). 8-K/6-K는 첨부 exhibit 중 컨퍼런스콜 녹취록으로 보이는 것이 있으면 그 원문을, 없어도 실적 관련 필링이면(8-K 항목 2.02, 또는 period_of_report가 채워진 6-K) 실적 보도자료 exhibit 원문을 캡처한다 - 그 외(합병/임원변경 등 실적과 무관한 8-K/6-K)는 메타데이터와 원문 링크만 캡처한다.
- 8-K 항목 코드는 사건의 주제를 나타낼 뿐 호재/악재 방향을 의미하지 않는다.
- 외국민간발행인(FPI)의 6-K는 국내 기업의 8-K와 제출 주기 및 내용 기준이 달라 직접 비교할 수 없다.
