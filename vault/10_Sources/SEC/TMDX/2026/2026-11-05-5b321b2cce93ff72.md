 PRESS RELEASE 

 TransMedics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results 

 Strong Revenue Generation Drives Increase in Full-Year 2026 Guidance; Recent Progress Positions Company for Continued Growth with Several Near- and Mid-Term Catalysts 

 Andover, Mass. – November 5, 2026 – TransMedics Group, Inc. (“TransMedics”) (Nasdaq: TMDX), a medical technology company that is transforming organ transplant therapy for patients with end-stage lung, heart, and liver failure, today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. 

 Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights 

 • 
 Total revenue of $203.0 million, up 41%, including organic revenue growth* of 35% 

 • 
 Product revenue of $113.4 million, up 29%, and service revenue of $89.6 million, up 60%, including organic service revenue growth* of 43% 

 • 
 Net income of $17.1 million or $0.46 per diluted share in the third quarter of 2026 

 • 
 Adjusted net income* of $18.9 million or $0.51 per diluted share in the third quarter of 2026 

 • 
 Raises full-year 2026 revenue guidance to between $764-$774 million, inclusive of approximately $14 million from PAD Aviation 

 Recent Business Highlights 

 • 
 Completed enrollment in Part A of the Next-Generation Organ Care System (OCS) ENHANCE Heart trial (September) and received U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) approval of a continued access protocol, allowing enrollment of an additional 90 patients (October) 

 • 
 Received Investigational Device Exemption (IDE) approvals from U.S. FDA for ENHANCE Heart Part B and DENOVO Lung trials, including use of TransMedics Controlled Hypothermic Organ Preservation System (CHOPS) as a control arm for both trials (October) 

 • 
 Awarded first European transplant logistics tender in Italy, commencing the company’s European transplant operations using PAD Aviation (October) 

 • 
 Appointed Fernando Araujo, former CFO of GE HealthCare's Advanced Imaging Solutions segment, as Chief Financial Officer (September) 

 “The third quarter of 2026 was the strongest in TransMedics’ history, with record revenue and record case volume. Importantly, we delivered these results despite typical third quarter seasonality and before any contribution from ENHANCE Part B or DENOVO.” said Waleed Hassanein, MD, President and Chief Executive Officer. “We enter 2027 with strong momentum and a robust set of growth catalysts. ENHANCE Heart Part B and DENOVO Lung target large, untapped heart and lung segments, with two shots on goal in each through OCS and CHOPS. In Europe, we plan to launch OCS NOP in Italy, the Netherlands and Belgium, building on our first European transplant logistics win. And OCS Kidney remains on track for first-in-human clinical experience in the second half of 2027, our path into the largest transplant market. Our strategy is clear. Our execution is proven. Our conviction has never been stronger. We are building the global platform to transform organ transplantation, and we strongly believe our best years are still ahead.” 

 

 

 
 

 

 
 

 
 
 
 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 

 

 Recent IDE Approvals 

 In October, TransMedics received full U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) IDE approvals for Part B of the OCS ENHANCE Heart trial and DENOVO Lung trial as the company further advances its OCS platform into new indications, seeks to capture new commercial opportunity, and addresses patient needs. 

 EU Expansion 

 TransMedics continues to expand its operations in the EU, including through winning its first logistics tender in Italy. This milestone supports the company’s growth strategy into key European markets and follows the establishment of its dedicated organ transplantation air logistics network through the recent strategic investment in PAD Aviation.

 Total revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $203.0 million, a 41% increase compared to $143.8 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was due primarily to increasing Liver and Heart volume through the National OCS Program (“NOP”) as well as additional revenue generated by TransMedics logistics services, and the contribution of PAD Aviation following the Company's strategic investment on July 1, 2026. 

 Gross margin was 58.5%, compared with 58.8% in the prior-year period. The year-over-year decrease primarily reflected a higher mix of service revenue, partly offset by reduced scrap, lower inventory adjustments and logistics efficiencies resulting from pricing improvements across both service and logistics operations, productivity gains from reduced supplemental lift utilization, lower ground transportation cost per mission, and improved leverage of fixed operating costs as volume increased. 

 

 

 
 

 

 
 

 
 
 
 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 

 

 Operating expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $90.5 million compared to $61.3 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase in operating expenses was driven by planned investment in OCS Kidney, Next-Generation OCS, and ENHANCE clinical programs, increased headcount to support development activities and company’s expansion, and infrastructure investments, including the US Headquarters and the addition of PAD Aviation. Third quarter operating expenses in 2026 included $12.1 million of stock compensation expense compared to $8.7 million of stock compensation expense in the third quarter of 2025. 

 Income from operations in the third quarter of 2026 was $28.1 million, compared to operating income of $23.3 million in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted income from operations in the third quarter of 2026 was $28.9 million compared to adjusted income from operations of $23.6 million in the third quarter of 2025. 

 Net income in the third quarter of 2026 was $17.1 million, or $0.46 per diluted share, compared to net income of $24.3 million, or $0.66 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted net income in the third quarter of 2026 was $18.9 million, or $0.51 per diluted share compared to adjusted net income of $24.5 million, or $0.67 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025. 

 Cash was $493.0 million as of September 30, 2026.

 About TransMedics Group, Inc. 

 TransMedics is the world’s leader in portable extracorporeal warm perfusion and assessment of donor organs for transplantation.  Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures 

 To supplement our consolidated financial statements, which are prepared and presented in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (“GAAP”), we disclose certain non-GAAP financial measures, including organic revenue, organic revenue growth, organic service revenue, organic service revenue growth, revenue guidance excluding PAD Aviation, adjusted income from operations, adjusted operating margin, adjusted net income, and adjusted diluted net income per common share. These non-GAAP financial measures are not calculated in accordance with GAAP, are not a substitute for, and should be considered supplemental to, GAAP financial measures. Our definitions of these non-GAAP measures may differ from similarly titled measures used by other companies, which may limit their usefulness for comparative purposes. 

 We use these non-GAAP financial measures for financial and operational decision-making and as a means to evaluate period-to-period comparisons. We believe the presentation of these measures is useful to both management and investors as they provide meaningful supplemental information with respect to our core operational performance and allow for greater transparency with respect to key metrics used by management in its financial and operational decision-making. 

 We report organic revenue, organic revenue growth, organic service revenue, organic service revenue growth and revenue guidance excluding PAD Aviation to provide management and investors with additional understanding and visibility into the underlying revenue trends of the Company’s established, ongoing operations, as well as provide insights into overall demand for its products and services. To calculate these measures, we exclude from revenue and service revenue the effect of the PAD Aviation transaction as there is no prior period comparable revenue. 

 To calculate adjusted income from operations, adjusted operating margin, adjusted net income and adjusted diluted net income per common share, we exclude certain charges (credits) from GAAP income from operations and GAAP net income, such as transaction-related costs, incremental amortization of intangible assets, ERP implementation costs, headquarters relocation costs, legal matters and discrete tax items. Amounts are presented after-tax using the company's statutory tax rate unless the amount is a significant unusual or infrequently occurring item in accordance with Financial Accounting Standards Board Accounting Standards Codification Topic 740-270-30, "General Methodology and Use of Estimated Annual Effective Tax Rate.”
