Nebius completes acquisition of Eigen

 Amsterdam, June 16, 2026 —
Nebius (Nasdaq: NBIS), the AI cloud company, today announced the closing of its acquisition of Eigen AI, a leading inference and model
optimization company.

 

The transaction was announced on May 1,
2026, and completed on June 10, 2026, following receipt of the required regulatory approvals and satisfaction of other customary closing
conditions.

 

About Nebius

 

Nebius, the AI cloud company, is building
the full-stack platform for developers and companies to take charge of their AI future — from data and model training to production
deployment. Founded on deep in-house technological expertise and operating at scale with a rapidly expanding global footprint, Nebius
serves startups and enterprises building AI products, agents and services worldwide.

 

Nebius is listed on Nasdaq (Nasdaq: NBIS)
and headquartered in Amsterdam.
