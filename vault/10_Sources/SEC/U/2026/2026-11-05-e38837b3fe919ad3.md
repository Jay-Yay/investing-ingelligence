 Unity Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results 

 SAN FRANCISCO, November 5, 2026 -- Unity (NYSE: U), the world’s leading game engine, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. 

 "Third-quarter performance was our strongest ever, with Vector growing 126% year-over-year and 40% quarter-over-quarter, and the first GAAP profitable quarter since our IPO," said Matt Bromberg, President and CEO of Unity. 

 "We are seeing the compounding impact of our flywheel strategy: more creators make more games on our platform, and more games fuel the need for more discovery through advertising."

 Third Quarter 2026 Results: 

 Total Revenue Highlights: 

 • Revenue was $622 million, compared to $471 million in the third quarter 2025. 

 • Grow Solutions revenue was $457 million, compared to $318 million in the third quarter 2025. 

 • Create Solutions revenue was $165 million, compared to $152 million in the third quarter 2025. 

 Profitability Highlights: 

 • GAAP net income was $122 million, with a margin of 20%; GAAP basic net income per share was $0.27; GAAP diluted net income per share was $0.25. 

 • Adjusted EBITDA was $257 million, with a margin of 41%; adjusted EPS was $0.44. 

 • Net cash provided by operating activities was $224 million; free cash flow was $220 million. 

 Revenue 

 Revenue was $622 million, up 32% year-over-year. Strategic revenue was $597 million, up 59% year-over-year. 

 Grow Solutions revenue was $457 million, up 44% year-over-year. The change was due to growth in the Unity Ads Network, driven by Unity Vector, partially offset by decreases in the ironSource Ads Network. 

 Create Solutions revenue was $165 million, up 8% year-over-year. The increase was driven by increases in subscription revenue, partially offset by decreases in cloud and hosting services revenue, driven by our portfolio reset in 2025. 

 Basic and Diluted Net Income (Loss) per share 

 Basic net income per share was $0.27, as compared to a basic net loss per share of $0.30 for the same period in 2025. 

 Diluted net income per share was $0.25, as compared to a diluted net loss per share of $0.30 for the same period in 2025. 

 Net Income (Loss) and Net Cash Provided by Operating Activities 

 Net income for the quarter was $122 million, compared to a net loss of $127 million in the third quarter of 2025. 

 Net income (loss) margin was 20%, compared to (27)% in the third quarter of 2025. 

 Net cash provided by operating activities for the quarter was $224 million, compared to $155 million in the third quarter of 2025.

 Adjusted EBITDA, Free Cash Flow, and Adjusted EPS 

 Adjusted EBITDA for the quarter was $257 million, with a margin of 41%, compared to $109 million in the third quarter of 2025, with a margin of 23%. The year-over-year improvement was driven by higher revenue and continued cost control. 

 Free cash flow for the quarter was $220 million, compared to $151 million in the third quarter of 2025. 

 Adjusted EPS for the quarter was $0.44, compared to $0.20 in the third quarter of 2025. 

 Liquidity 

 As of September 30, 2026, our cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash was $2,651 million, an increase of $587 million, compared with $2,064 million as of December 31, 2025. This increase was primarily driven by our operations.

 About Non-GAAP Financial Measures 

 To supplement our consolidated financial statements prepared and presented in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (GAAP) we use certain non-GAAP financial measures, as described below, to evaluate our ongoing operations and for internal planning and forecasting purposes. We believe the following non-GAAP measures are useful in evaluating our operating performance. We are presenting these non-GAAP financial measures because we believe, when taken collectively, they may be helpful to investors because they provide consistency and comparability with past financial performance. 

 However, non-GAAP financial measures have limitations in their usefulness to investors because they have no standardized meaning prescribed by GAAP and are not prepared under any comprehensive set of accounting rules or principles. In addition, other companies, including companies in our industry, may calculate similarly-titled non-GAAP financial measures differently or may use other measures to evaluate their performance, all of which could reduce the usefulness of our non-GAAP financial measures as tools for comparison. As a result, our non-GAAP financial measures are presented for supplemental informational purposes only and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for our consolidated financial statements presented in accordance with GAAP. 

 We define adjusted EBITDA as GAAP net income or loss excluding stock-based compensation expense, amortization and impairment of intangible assets, depreciation expense, gain on sale of business, restructuring and reorganization costs, interest expense, interest income and other income (expense), net and the provision for income taxes. We define adjusted EBITDA margin as adjusted EBITDA as a percentage of revenue. We define adjusted gross profit as GAAP gross profit excluding stock-based compensation expense, amortization and impairment of intangible assets, depreciation expense, and restructuring and reorganization costs. We define adjusted gross margin as adjusted gross profit as a percentage of revenue. 

 We define adjusted cost of revenue as GAAP cost of revenue, excluding stock-based compensation expense, amortization and impairment of intangible assets, depreciation expense, and restructuring and reorganization costs. We define adjusted research and development expense as GAAP research and development expense, excluding stock-based compensation expense, amortization and impairment of intangible assets, depreciation expense, gain on sale of business, and restructuring and reorganization costs. We define adjusted sales and marketing expense as GAAP sales and marketing expense, excluding stock-based compensation expense, amortization and impairment of intangible assets, depreciation expense, gain on sale of business, and restructuring and reorganization costs. We define adjusted general and administrative expense as GAAP general and administrative expense excluding stock-based compensation expense, depreciation expense, impairment of intangible assets, gain on sale of business, and restructuring and reorganization costs. We define free cash flow as net cash provided by operating activities less cash used for purchases of property and equipment. 

 We define adjusted EPS as GAAP net income or loss excluding stock-based compensation expense, amortization and impairment of intangible assets, depreciation expense, gain on sale of business, restructuring and reorganization costs, and the income tax impact of the preceding adjustments (cumulatively "adjusted net income"), increased by the tax effected impacts from any relevant dilutive securities, divided by the diluted weighted-average outstanding shares. The effective tax rate used in calculating adjusted EPS is estimated for each period, based on the net income or loss adjusted for the items noted above, and may differ from the effective rate used in our financial statements. Shares of common stock that are excluded in our calculation of GAAP diluted net loss per share due to their antidilutive impact on such calculations, are included in the diluted weighted average outstanding shares used in our calculation of adjusted EPS, to the extent they have a dilutive impact on adjusted EPS given the adjusted net income in each period.
