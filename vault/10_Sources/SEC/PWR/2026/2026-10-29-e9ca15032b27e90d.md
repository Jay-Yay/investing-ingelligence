 QUANTA SERVICES REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS 

 Third Quarter Consolidated Revenues of $12.0 Billion* 

 Third Quarter GAAP Diluted EPS of $4.55* and Adjusted Diluted EPS of $6.06* 

 Net Income Attributable to Common Stock of $694.9 Million* 

 Adjusted EBITDA of $1.4 Billion* 

 Cash Flow From Operations of $1.5 Billion* and Free Cash Flow of $1.2 Billion* 

 Remaining Performance Obligations (RPO) of $41.8 Billion* and Total Backlog of $63.7 Billion* 

 Strong Performance Drives Significant Increase In 2026 Financial Expectations Across All Metrics 

 * = Record quarterly or record third quarter result 

 HOUSTON – October 29, 2026 - Quanta Services, Inc. (NYSE: PWR) today announced results for the three months ended
September 30, 2026. Revenues in the third quarter of 2026 were $12.01 billion compared to revenues of $7.63 billion in the third quarter of 2025, and net income attributable to common stock was $694.9 million, or $4.55 per
diluted share, in the third quarter of 2026 compared to net income attributable to common stock of $339.4 million, or $2.24 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted diluted earnings per share attributable to common stock was
$6.06 for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $3.33 for the third quarter of 2025. 
 
“Quanta delivered another exceptional quarter, with
third-quarter results that again exceeded expectations. Revenue, adjusted EBITDA and adjusted diluted earnings per share all achieved strong double-digit growth, cash flow was robust and total backlog reached a record $63.7 billion. These
results demonstrate the power of our solutions-based operating model, as well as the execution certainty our self-perform capabilities deliver for customers every day. Given this outperformance and the expected incremental strength in the fourth
quarter, we are again significantly increasing our full-year 2026 financial expectations across all metrics,” said Duke Austin, President and Chief Executive Officer of Quanta Services.

“The breadth of our portfolio, combined with the fungibility of our skilled workforce across multiple
end markets, provides a significant competitive advantage. This flexibility allows us to strategically deploy resources where demand is strongest, mitigate exposure to individual market cycles and deliver more consistent, predictable outcomes for
our customers and stockholders. With demand for our services broadening across utility, power generation, industrial and technology customers, we are pacing ahead of the multi-year growth targets we outlined at our 2026 Investor Day, and our
conviction in Quanta’s ability to compound profitable growth over the long term continues to strengthen. We believe there is opportunity to deliver strong double-digit adjusted EPS growth in 2027, supported by continued backlog growth.”

 
Certain items that impacted Quanta’s results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025 are reflected as adjustments in
the calculation of Quanta’s adjusted net income attributable to common stock, adjusted diluted earnings per share attributable to common stock and adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP financial measures). These
items are described in the accompanying tables reconciling adjusted net income attributable to common stock, EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA to net income attributable to common stock and adjusted diluted earnings per share attributable to common stock
to diluted earnings per share attributable to common stock. Quanta completed five acquisitions in the first nine months of 2026 and eight acquisitions during the full year 2025, and the results of the acquired businesses are included in
Quanta’s consolidated results from the respective acquisition dates. For further information on the items that impacted comparability of 2026 and 2025, see the footnotes in the accompanying tables presenting Supplemental Segment Data and
reconciliations of EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted net income attributable to common stock and adjusted diluted earnings per share attributable to common stock (non-GAAP financial measures) to their
comparable GAAP financial measures. 
 
 RECENT HIGHLIGHTS 
 
 

 

 
 • 
 
 
 Completed Two Acquisitions - In September and October 2026, Quanta completed two acquisitions that
strengthen its front-end, fabrication and supply chain capabilities. The first is a Houston, Texas-based energy services provider with approximately 320 employees that offers engineering and design,
fabrication, automation and controls, and engineering, procurement and construction services, and is included in the Underground and Infrastructure segment. The second is a Fort Worth, Texas-based vertically integrated manufacturer of steel
monopoles and lighting structures, primarily for utility transmission and distribution applications, with approximately 270 employees, which will primarily be included in the Electric segment. Aggregate consideration, net of cash acquired, was
approximately $245 million, consisting of approximately $96 million in cash paid or payable and approximately $149 million in Quanta common stock. The two newly announced acquisitions are not expected to contribute materially to
Quanta’s full-year 2026 financial results. 
 
 
 

 

 
 • 
 
 
 Enhanced Liquidity and Financial Flexibility - In August 2026, Quanta issued $2.00 billion aggregate
principal amount of senior notes. In July 2026, Quanta increased the aggregate revolving commitments under its senior credit facility from $2.80 billion to $2.98 billion and extended the maturity date for revolving loans from July 31,
2030 to July 31, 2031. Effective August 8, 2026, Quanta increased the maximum amount of its unsecured commercial paper program from $2.80 billion to $2.98 billion. On September 1, 2026, Quanta prepaid the full
$618.8 million outstanding balance of the term loan under its senior credit facility.

 Published 2025 Sustainability Report - In September 2026, Quanta published its 2025 Sustainability
Report , which provides transparency around Quanta’s sustainability strategy and the company’s continued progress in 2025. Titled “Craft + Culture,” the report highlights how Quanta’s craft-skilled workforce and the
investment in training, safety and leadership development behind it position the company to meet growing demand for reliable power, resilient infrastructure and modern energy systems.

 Appointed Ellen Rubin to Board of Directors - In September 2026, Ellen Rubin joined Quanta’s Board
of Directors. Ms. Rubin currently serves as an Operating Partner at Glasswing Ventures LLC, a venture capital firm focused on AI and frontier technologies, and as a director of Allegion plc. She brings executive leadership and public company
board experience, as well as deep technology expertise, including AI and cloud computing innovation, technology strategy and cybersecurity risk management. 
 
 

 RESULTS FOR THE NINE MONTHS ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2026 

Revenues in the nine months ended September 30, 2026 were $29.45 billion compared to revenues of $20.64 billion in the nine months ended
September 30, 2025, and net income attributable to common stock was $1.37 billion, or $8.97 per diluted share, in the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared to net income attributable to common stock of $712.9 million, or
$4.72 per diluted share, in the nine months ended September 30, 2025. Adjusted diluted earnings per share attributable to common stock was $12.98 for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $7.59 for the nine months ended
September 30, 2025. 
 
 FULL-YEAR 2026 OUTLOOK 

Prior to the Company’s conference call, management will post a summary of Quanta’s updated 2026 guidance expectations with additional commentary in
the “News and Events” and “Financial Info” areas of the Investor Relations section of Quanta’s website at http://investors.quantaservices.com . 

The long-term outlook for Quanta’s business is positive. However, weather, regulatory, permitting, supply chain challenges and other factors affecting
project timing and execution have impacted, and may impact in the future, Quanta’s financial results. Additionally, we continue to consider future uncertainty associated with overall challenges to the domestic and global economy, including
inflation, interest rates and potential recessionary economic conditions. Quanta’s financial outlook for revenues, margins and earnings reflects management’s effort to align these uncertainties with the backlog the Company is executing
on and the opportunities expected to materialize during the remainder of 2026. 
 
The following forward-looking statements are based on current
expectations, and actual results may differ materially, as described below in Cautionary Statement About Forward-Looking Statements and Information . For the full year ending December 31, 2026, Quanta now expects revenues to range between
$41.3 billion and $41.6 billion and net income attributable to common stock to range between $1.92 billion and $1.98 billion. Quanta also now expects diluted earnings per share attributable to common stock to range between $12.57
and $12.97 and adjusted diluted earnings per share attributable to common stock to range between $17.90 and $18.30. Quanta now expects EBITDA to range between $3.99 billion and $4.09 billion and adjusted EBITDA to range between
$4.40 billion and $4.49 billion. Additionally, for the full year ending December 31, 2026, Quanta now expects net cash provided by operating activities to range between $3.45 billion and $4.00 billion and free cash flow (a non-GAAP financial measure) to range between $2.50 billion and $3.00 billion.

 Remaining Performance Obligations and Backlog (a non-GAAP financial measure)
 
 
Quanta’s remaining performance obligations represent management’s estimate of consolidated revenues that are expected to be realized from
the remaining portion of firm orders under fixed price contracts not yet completed or for which work has not yet begun as of such dates and, to a lesser extent, from certain unit-priced contracts with more than an insignificant amount of partially
completed units. For purposes of calculating remaining performance obligations, Quanta includes all estimated revenues attributable to consolidated joint ventures and variable interest entities, revenues from funded and unfunded portions of
government contracts to the extent they are reasonably expected to be realized, and revenues from change orders and claims to the extent management believes additional contract revenues will be earned and are deemed probable of collection. 

Quanta has also historically disclosed its backlog, a measure commonly used in its industry but not recognized under GAAP. Quanta believes this measure
enables management to more effectively forecast its future capital needs and results and better identify future operating trends that may not otherwise be apparent. Quanta believes this measure is also useful for investors in forecasting
Quanta’s future results and comparing Quanta to its competitors. Quanta’s remaining performance obligations, as described above, are a component of its backlog calculation, which also includes estimated orders under master service
agreements (MSAs), including estimated renewals, and certain non-fixed price contracts. Quanta’s methodology for determining backlog may not be comparable to the methodologies used by other companies.

 
Estimates of the timing of revenue recognition of remaining performance obligations are subject to change based on, among other things, project
accelerations; project cancellations or delays, including but not limited to those caused by commercial issues, permitting and regulatory requirements, natural disasters, emergencies and adverse weather conditions; and final acceptance of change
orders by customers. These factors can cause revenues to be realized in periods and at levels that are different than originally projected.
