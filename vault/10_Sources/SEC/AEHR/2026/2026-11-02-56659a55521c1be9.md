 Aehr Test Systems Reports Record First Quarter Fiscal 2027 Revenue of $33.0 Million; Raises Full-Year Revenue Outlook to $150 Million, Triple Fiscal 2026 Revenue 
 
 
 
 Strong Quarterly Bookings of $46.7 Million; Effective Backlog Reaches $109.3 Million 
 
 
 
 FREMONT, Calif., November 2, 2026 – Aehr Test Systems (NASDAQ: AEHR), a leading provider of test and burn-in solutions for semiconductor devices used in artificial intelligence (AI), silicon photonics, data center, automotive, and industrial applications, today announced financial results for its first quarter of fiscal 2027 ended September 25, 2026.
 
 
 
 Fiscal First Quarter Financial Results: 
 
 
 
● Record revenue was $33.0 million, up 200% from $11.0 million in the first quarter of fiscal 2026 and 75% sequentially.
 
● GAAP gross margin was 47.5%, and non-GAAP gross margin was 48.5%.
 
● GAAP net income was $7.2 million, or $0.21 per diluted share, compared with a GAAP net loss of $(2.1) million, or $(0.07) per diluted share, in the first quarter of fiscal 2026.
 
● Non-GAAP net income, which excludes stock-based compensation and acquisition-related adjustments, was $9.4 million, or $0.28 per diluted share, compared with non-GAAP net income of $0.2 million, or $0.01 per diluted share, in the first quarter of fiscal 2026. Non-GAAP net income increased approximately 163% sequentially.
 
● Bookings were $46.7 million, representing a book-to-bill ratio of approximately 1.4.
 
● Backlog was $100.9 million as of September 25, 2026. Effective backlog, including bookings received after quarter end, was $109.3 million as of November 1, 2026.
 
● Cash, cash equivalents and restricted cash totaled $105.1 million as of September 25, 2026, compared to $116.5 million as of May 29, 2026.
 
 
 
An explanation of the use of non-GAAP financial measures and a reconciliation to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measures are included in the accompanying financial tables.

 About Aehr Test Systems 
 
 
 
Headquartered in Fremont, California, Aehr Test Systems is a leading provider of test solutions for testing, burning in, and stabilizing semiconductor devices in wafer-level, singulated die, and package-level form, and has installed thousands of systems worldwide. Increasing quality, reliability, safety, and security needs of semiconductors used across multiple applications, including advanced AI processors, silicon photonics, data and telecommunications infrastructure, electric vehicles, electric vehicle charging infrastructure, solar and wind power, computing, and solid-state memory and storage are driving additional test requirements, incremental capacity needs, and new opportunities for Aehr’s products and solutions. Aehr has developed and introduced several innovative products including the FOX-P TM families of test and burn-in systems and the FOX WaferPak TM Aligner, FOX WaferPak Contactor, FOX DiePak ® Carrier and FOX DiePak Loader. The FOX-XP and FOX-NP systems are full-wafer contact and singulated die/module test and burn-in systems that can test, burn in, and stabilize a wide range of devices such as leading-edge silicon carbide-based and other power semiconductors, 2D and 3D sensors used in mobile phones, tablets, and other computing devices, memory semiconductors, processors, microcontrollers, systems-on-a-chip, and photonics and integrated optical devices. The FOX-CP system is a low-cost single-wafer compact test solution for logic, memory and photonic devices and the newest addition to the FOX-P product family. The FOX WaferPak Contactor contains a unique full-wafer contactor capable of testing wafers up to 300mm, enabling IC manufacturers to perform test, burn-in, and stabilization of full wafers on FOX-P systems. The FOX DiePak Carrier allows testing, burn-in, and stabilization of singulated bare die and modules up to 1024 devices in parallel per DiePak on the FOX-NP and FOX-XP systems up to nine DiePaks at a time. Through its acquisition of Incal Technology, Inc., Aehr added a line of high-power package-level reliability/burn-in test solutions for AI semiconductor manufacturers, including its ultra-high-power Sonoma family of test solutions for AI accelerators, GPUs, and high-performance computing (HPC) processors, which position Aehr within the rapidly growing AI market as a turnkey provider of reliability and test solutions that span from engineering to high-volume production. For more information, please visit Aehr Test Systems’ website at www.aehr.com.

- 10-K/10-Q/20-F는 primaryDocument HTML에서 텍스트를 추출해 전문을 캡처한다(태그만 제거한 단순 변환이라 표 구조가 완벽히 보존되진 않는다). 8-K/6-K는 첨부 exhibit 중 컨퍼런스콜 녹취록으로 보이는 것이 있으면 그 원문을, 없어도 실적 관련 필링이면(8-K 항목 2.02, 또는 period_of_report가 채워진 6-K) 실적 보도자료 exhibit 원문을 캡처한다 - 그 외(합병/임원변경 등 실적과 무관한 8-K/6-K)는 메타데이터와 원문 링크만 캡처한다.
- 8-K 항목 코드는 사건의 주제를 나타낼 뿐 호재/악재 방향을 의미하지 않는다.
- 외국민간발행인(FPI)의 6-K는 국내 기업의 8-K와 제출 주기 및 내용 기준이 달라 직접 비교할 수 없다.
