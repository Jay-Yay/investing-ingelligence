[BOJ 성명서] BOJ Statement on Monetary Policy (2026-10-30)

1 
October 30, 202 6 
Bank of Japan 
 
Statement on Monetary Policy 
 
1. Guideline for money market operations (an 8-1 majority vote) [Note] 
At the Monetary Policy Meeting  (MPM) held today, the Policy Board of the Bank of Japan 
decided to set the following guideline for money market operations for the intermeeting period:  
The Bank will encourage the uncollateralized overnight call rate to remain at around 1.25 
percent. 
2. Change in the pace of sales of exchange-traded funds (ETFs) (a unanimous vote) 
At the MPM held in September 2025, the Bank , drawing on  its experience of the smooth 
conduct of sales of the "stocks purchased from financial institutions " through July 2025,  
decided to sell its ETF holdings for the time being at a scale generally equivalent to that of the 
sales of the "stocks purchased from financial institutions " (the sales amount accounted for 
about 0.05 percent of the trading value  in the market at the time), and set the pace of sales at 
about 330 billion yen per year (on a book value basis). 
The Bank started its sales of ETFs in January 2026 , and has been conducting these sales  
smoothly while avoiding a major impact  on the market. Meanwhile, the trading value in the 
stock market has been expanding. Given these developments, at the MPM held today, the Bank 
decided to sell its ETF holdings at a scale of about 0.1 percent of the trading value in the market, 
and set the pace of sales at about 860 billion yen per year (on a book value basis).1,2,3,4 
 
1 The pace of sales is c alculated using the trading value in the Tokyo Stock Exchange Prime Market  from 
January to September 2026 and the market-to-book ratio of the ETFs sold by the Bank during the same period. 
If the annual sales amount is converted to market value using the same ratio, it would be about 2,460 billion 
yen. 
2 The change will be implemented in January 2027. 
3 A temporary adjustment in the sales amount or a suspension of sales may be made within the range of the 
annual sales pace in response to changes in financial market conditions. (This remains the same as before.) 
4 The pace of sales of Japan real estate investment trusts  (J-REITs) will remain at about 5 billion yen per 
year (on a book value basis).

2 
 
[Note] V oting for the action: UEDA Kazuo, HIMINO Ryozo, UCHIDA Shinichi, TAMURA Naoki, KOEDA 
Junko, MASU Kazuyuki, ASADA Toichiro, and SA TO Ayano. V oting against the action: TAKATA 
Hajime. Takata Hajime considered that, with underlying CPI inflation having reached the price stability 
target, the situation had shifted to a new phase in which the Bank needs to step up the pace of bringing 
the policy interest rate closer to the neutral interest rate so that it could make  nimble responses. He 
proposed that the Bank set the guideline for money market operations as follows: the Bank would 
encourage the uncollateralized overnight call rate to remain at around 1. 5 percent. The proposal was 
defeated by a majority vote.
