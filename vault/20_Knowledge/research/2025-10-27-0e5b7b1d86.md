Key views on the market
Directionally cautious: We have reduced directional exposures in stocks and bonds and lowered overall portfolio risk as conflicting macro and 
positioning signals make us cautious heading into the summer. Consistent with our investment philsophy, we focus on cross-country relative 
value opportunities on the back of monetary policy shifts, fiscal developments, and inflation divergences.
Japanese reflation: We have added to Japanese equity longs versus other Asian markets. Strong growth, trade competitiveness, and a set of 
ambitious fiscal plans from the Takaichi government provide a strong backdrop for Topix earnings in the coming quarter.
Draghi Report fiscal: We see the Iranian conflict as catalyst for a Eurozone fiscal expansion, with Germany’s ambitious reform program - pension, 
labor market, industrial and tax reforms - supporting stronger growth and higher bond issuance. The portfolio is short Eurozone duration relative to 
UK, Polish, and Swiss markets, as we believe common currency bond markets are still underpricing upside risks to the fiscal ( and inflation) outlook.
