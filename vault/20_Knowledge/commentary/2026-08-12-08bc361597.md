Russia Daily Oil Output Almost Million Barrels Below OPEC+ Quota
Russia’s crude-only production lagged behind its OPEC+ quota by almost a million barrels a day in July as Ukraine attacked the nation’s oil infrastructure almost daily.
Producers pumped an average of 8.887 million barrels a day of crude last month, according to a monthly report by the Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries. While that’s a drop of just 6,000 barrels a day from the average level in June, which was revised down, July’s daily production is significantly below Russia’s target level of 9.824 million barrels for the month under its agreement with its OPEC+ allies.
