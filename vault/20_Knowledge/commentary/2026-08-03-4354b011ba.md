### [News] Samsung Foundry Eyes 2H26 Full Utilization as HBM4 Base Dies and U.S. Orders Fuel Turnaround Hopes

(https://vo.la/AXdnEHF)

[News] Samsung Foundry Eyes 2H26 Full Utilization as HBM4 Base Dies and U.S. Orders Fuel Turnaround Hopes
At last week’s earnings call, Samsung surprised the market with a strong performance, driven almost entirely by its memory business, which contributed 99.7% of total operating profit. While the foundry unit remained in the red, a turnaround could come sooner than expected, with Chosun Biz reporting that Samsung Foundry aims to reach 100% capacity utilization by the second half of this year, up from the current 70%–80% range.
According to Newsis, Samsung’s non-memory businesses, including Foundry and System LSI, are estimated to have posted a loss of around KRW 600 billion in 2Q. The improvement has fueled expectations of an earlier-than-expected turnaround, with some industry observers speculating that profitability could return within this year, ahead of Han Jin-man’s previously cited 2028 timeline, the report adds.
The most bullish outlook may come from HuffPost Korea, which cited an analyst report projecting that Samsung Foundry could return to profitability as early as the third quarter. The forecast was based on the ramp-up of HBM4 base die production and increased output of Samsung’s in-house Exynos application processors, according to the report.
Chosun Biz attributes Samsung Foundry’s utilization recovery to stronger demand for advanced products, particularly HBM base dies as well as growing orders from major U.S. tech customers. The improvement marks a sharp reversal from last year, when utilization at key nodes such as 4nm and 5nm fell below 50%, according to the report.
Samsung’s 2nm Gains Traction Despite Yield Hurdles
Still, the recovery of advanced nodes and further yield improvements will determine whether Samsung Foundry can sustain its turnaround momentum. According to Chosun Biz, Samsung needs to raise 2nm yields to around 70% to secure large-scale mass production contracts, with current yields estimated at roughly 60%.
Despite the remaining 2nm yield challenges, Samsung is gaining traction among global tech giants. According to News1, Samsung is expanding advanced-node engagements with major customers such as Broadcom, with 2nm order volume expected to more than double this year from last year’s level.
The Information also reported earlier that while Google plans to have TSMC manufacture the main computing die for its next-generation TPU, codenamed “Icefish,” Samsung could be tapped to produce an I/O-related component that connects the chip to memory using its 2nm technology.
Meanwhile, Samsung is already reshaping its business mix. As noted by Chosun Biz, advanced-node processes are expected to account for more than 50% of the company’s foundry revenue this year, while AI and HPC-related sales are projected to rise from the high-teens percentage range last year to over 30%.

### Intel EMIB yields hit target, MediaTek eyes 2028 ASIC ramp

(https://vo.la/GMTXgUj)

MediaTek said at its latest earnings call that its second-generation data-center ASIC product remains on schedule for mass production in 2028, and that the Intel embedded multi-die interconnect bridge (EMIB) advanced packaging process it uses has already reached a very good yield level. MediaTek also said its first-generation product remains on track for volume ramp-up in the fourth quarter of 2026 and is expected to generate US$2 billion in revenue.
MediaTek's latest outlook underscored its confidence in the business. The company raised its 2027 serviceable available market (SAM) to US$80 billion, while lifting its market-share target from a range of 10%-15% to a range of 15%-20%.
On EMIB progress, MediaTek CEO Rick Tsai said back-end packaging is a critical step for a complex ASIC and that the company has worked closely with suppliers on substrate yield improvement, capacity supply, and production cycle times, managing each operational detail one by one.
Tsai said these technologies are highly challenging, but they are also the source of chip performance and total cost of ownership (TCO) advantages. He added that EMIB has made good progress in yield improvement and technical maturity, enough to keep the 2028 mass-production schedule on track. He also noted that Unimicron Technology, a major Taiwanese substrate maker, had previously made a more positive statement on EMIB, which he said reinforced the credibility of MediaTek's comments.
When asked whether the company needed to reserve TSMC chip-on-wafer-on-substrate (CoWoS) capacity as a backup for the second-generation project, CFO David Ku said switching packaging technologies takes time. He added that based on the positive data and results from the second project so far, MediaTek is confident it can meet the mass-production target on schedule. That also suggests the current EMIB path for the second ASIC no longer needs a fallback plan.
Cloud AI demand and 2028 second-gen ASICs
Strong technical progress and robust demand in the cloud AI market are giving MediaTek the confidence to keep raising its outlook. Tsai said the upgraded 2027 SAM of US$80 billion covers AI accelerators only, excluding CPUs, network switches, and high-bandwidth memory (HBM).
That means MediaTek still sees room for SAM expansion if it can win supply contracts for peripheral chips in addition to AI accelerators.
Investors are also watching the outlook for the period after the second-generation ASIC enters mass production in 2028. Tsai said the second-generation ASIC will deliver a major improvement in performance per TCO and performance per watt. With both generations expected to be in mass production by then, the 2028 SAM will be significantly higher than in 2027, with market share also rising sharply.
Asked whether MediaTek could become the customer's main supplier, Tsai was more cautious. He said the company will continue to work steadily and build customers' trust, with the goal of winning a share that is "better than deserved."
On the progress of other major customer projects, MediaTek said it cannot disclose unconfirmed projects for now, but will announce them once everything is settled. It added that its two projects with its main customer are already enough to keep the company busy through 2028 and beyond, and it remains highly confident in the business's medium- and long-term prospects.
Another key point was the board's approval of a US$5 billion flexible financing budget. Ku said MediaTek already has more than US$7 billion in cash on hand and a fairly strong balance sheet, and that the extra budget is a flexible framework to respond to two fast-changing industry variables: overall supply-chain conditions and the possible business models for AI ASICs. When needed, the flexible funds can be layered on top of existing financial resources and deployed urgently.
Tsai also confirmed that the budget could be used to support key suppliers' capacity expansion to strengthen supply-chain ties. That suggests IC design companies are now also investing in upstream capacity expansion to secure ASIC production slots, highlighting the intensity of competition for ASIC capacity. For Taiwan substrate makers and the broader advanced packaging supply chain, that is also positive news.

### 범용 D램, 7월도 최고가 갱신...3분기 20% 뛴다 < 반도체 < 기사본문 - 디일렉(THE ELEC)

(https://vo.la/Azikkdd)

7월 범용 D램과 낸드플래시 메모리 가격이 최고가를 기록했다.
3일 시장조사업체 D램익스체인지에 따르면 PC용 범용 제품 DDR4 8Gb의 7월 평균 가격은 24달러로, 전월(21달러) 대비 14.29% 증가했다. 2016년 6월 집계 이래 최고가다.
메모리카드와 USB향 범용 낸드 멀티레벨셀(MLC) 128Gb 7월 평균가는 30.05달러로 집계됐다. 30달러를 돌파한 것은 2015년 8월 집계 이래 최초다.
3분기 가격도 오름세가 지속될 전망이다.
시장조사업체 트렌드포스에 따르면 일부 DDR4 공급 업체는 PC 제조사와 지난달 말 계약가격 협상을 마무리했다. 트렌드포스는 3분기 DDR4 공급가가 전분기 대비 15~20% 상승할 것으로 예상했다. 다만 45~50% 상승폭을 보인 2분기보다 상승폭은 적다.
낸드도 상승하지만 상승폭은 크게 둔화될 것으로 보인다. 7월 범용 낸드 가격은 전월 대비 4.26% 올랐다. 두자릿수 상승세를 보였던 올해 초와는 대비된다. 트렌드포스는 시장에서 이미 많이 올라간 MLC 가격에 대한 반발이라고 분석했다.
